Trading Mein Kitna Pesa Lagana Chahiye?

Sab se aam sawal yeh hota hai: "trading shuru karne ke liye kitna paisa chahiye?" Jawaab sirf rakam nahi — balki kitna risk bardasht ho sakta hai aur aap kis cheez ki practice kar rahe hain.

Is guide mein hum batate hain ke chhoti rakam se kaise shuru karein, kaunsi savings use karni chahiye, aur "seedha ghar ki saving" kyun ghatak hoti hai.

1. Pehla Qanoon: Sirf Woh Paise Lagayein Jo Khone Par Farq Na Pade

Trading ka sab se pehla rule yeh hai: aapka trading capital woh paisa hai jiska 100% nuqsan hone par bhi aapki zindagi, kiraya, EMI aur ghar ka kharcha mutasir na ho.

  • Ghar ki emergency savings trading mein NA lagayein
  • Loan ya credit card se trading bilkul nahi
  • Jo paise 6 mahine andar chahiye hon (fees, fees, shaadi, ilaj) woh nahi
  • Trading capital alag account/rakam hon — ghar ke paise se

Yeh rule isliye hai ke pehle saal sab seekh rahe hain — us dauran nuqsan hone ka chance sab se zyada hota hai.

2. Kitna Capital — Market Ke Hisab Se

Har market ka apna minimum hai jo practical (fees, lot size, diversification) ke mutabiq chalti hai:

MarketChhoti shuruatComfortable start
Crypto (spot)$10-50$200-500
Forex (micro lot)$50-100$300-1000
Stocks (PSX/US)Chand hazaar rupees$300-500
Futures/leverage$500+$1000+

Lekin asal hisaab yeh hai: agar aapke paas $200 hain aur aap 1% risk karte hain, to har trade ka risk sirf $2 hai — jo fees ke baad lagbhag kuch nahi. Capital itna chahiye ke 1% bhi kuch mayne rakhta ho.

3. 1% Risk Rule Aur Position Sizing

Sahi capital ka asal hisaab risk management se nikalta hai:

  • Har trade par capital ka 1% (max 2%) risk — risk-reward kam az kam 1:2
  • Position size aise set karein ke stop-loss hit hone par sirf 1% jaye — position size calculator
  • Agar capital $500 hai, 1% = $5 — 10 trade agar haar bhi jayein, capital 90% bacha hai

Isi hisaab se pata chalta hai ke $50 par trading karna "practice" nahi, sirf lottery khelna hai. Chhota capital = chhota risk = seekhne mein zyada waqt.

4. Bara Capital Kyun? (Compounding Ka Khel)

Capital barhne se faida yeh hota hai ke same percentage ka faida b bara amount par banta hai. Misal (1% daily risk rule ke sath, mahana 5% realistic growth):

CapitalMahana 5%Saal baad
$200$10~$358
$1,000$50~$1,796
$5,000$250~$8,979

Faida yeh hai ke learning phase mein aap chhote kharche par seekhte hain, aur jab skill ban jaye to barha capital lagate hain. Ulta tareeqa (pehle bara paisa, baad mein seekhna) aksar tabaahi karta hai. Drawdown ka concept samajh lein — badhne se pehle giraavat ka darwaza bhi kholta hai.

5. Leverage Khatra Barhata Hai — Capital Ka Khel Na Banaye

Leverage aap ko chhote capital se bara position deta hai — lekin nuqsan bhi utna hi bara karta hai. Log $200 se $20,000 ka position khol lete hain aur ek chhoti chalat sab khatam kar deti hai.

  • Shuru mein leverage 2x-5x se zyada nahi
  • Leverage = capital ka multiplier, skill ka nahi
  • Futures mein liquidation price seedha apne account par likha hota hai — woh dekh kar trade karein

Jitna zyada leverage, utna kam waqt. Pehle skill, phir levarj — na ke ulta.

6. Pehla Deposit Karne Se Pehle Checklist

  1. Demo par 4-6 hafte ki trading history hai
  2. Apni strategy likhi hui hai (entry, stop, target, size)
  3. Emergency savings alag mehfooz hai
  4. Amount ka 100% hone par bhi ghar chalta rahega — yeh confirm hai
  5. Broker/exchange verified hai — scam check
  6. Journal setup hai — TradeEd journal

In mein se ek bhi missing ho to deposit rok lein — paise kabhi wapas nahi aate, practice kal bhi ho sakti hai.

Aap Ke Sawalat (FAQ)

Kya main 1000 rupees se trading shuru kar sakta hoon?

Technically haan (crypto mein), lekin 1% risk par 10 rupees per trade — fees aur emotions ke sath yeh practice nahi rehta. Chhoti rakam par demo par seekhein aur savings barhti jaane par capital barhayein.

Kya ghar ki saving trading mein lagani chahiye?

Bilkul nahi. Trading capital woh paisa hona chahiye jiska poora nuqsan ho kar bhi zindagi na badalay. Ghar ki saving, emergency fund aur loan kabhi trading mein nahi lagana chahiye.

Trading ke liye loan lena theek hai?

Nahi. Loan par trading ka pressure double karta hai — nuqsan hote hi jazbaat barh jate hain aur galtiyan tez hoti hain. Skill banne se pehle loan ki trading aksar tabaahi hoti hai.

Capital kitni tezi se barh sakta hai?

Realistic target mahana 2-5% hai. Daily double, guaranteed returns jaise dawe fraud hain. Capital tab barhayein jab aapke paas kam az kam 6 mahine ka consistent record ho.
