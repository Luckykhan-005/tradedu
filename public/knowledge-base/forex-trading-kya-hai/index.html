Forex Trading Kya Hai?

Forex (Foreign Exchange) duniya ka sab se bara financial market hai — jahan mulkon ki currencies ki aapas mein khareed-o-farokht hoti hai. Har din trillions dollar ki value yahan trade hoti hai.

Is guide mein aap seekhenge ke forex kaam kaise karta hai, pips-lot-spread kya hain, aur Pakistan se forex trading kaise shuru ki jaa sakti hai — Roman Urdu mein.

1. Forex Market Kya Hai?

Forex mein aap kisi mulk ki currency ko doosri mulk ki currency se trade karte hain. Yeh hamesha joron (pairs) mein hota hai — misal ke taur par EUR/USD ka matlab: Euro khareedna aur saath hi US Dollar bechna.

  • Major pairs: EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, USD/CHF — sab se zyada trade hote hain
  • Minor/Exotic pairs: USD/PKR jaise — kam liquidity, zyada spread
  • 24/5 khula: hafta se juma tak, duniya ke har waqt
  • Rozana $7.5 trillion se zyada ki trading — stocks aur crypto se kai guna bara

Currency ki qeemat aapas ke taluq (relative value) se chalti hai — jab dollar mazboot hota hai, EUR/USD girta hai.

2. Pips, Lot Aur Spread Kya Hai?

Forex mein teen alfaaz aksar aate hain — yeh samajh lein to hisaab aasaan:

  • Pip: qeemat ka chhota tareen farq (aksar 4th decimal — EUR/USD 1.0850 se 1.0851 = 1 pip)
  • Lot: trade ki miqdar. 1 standard lot = 100,000 units, mini lot = 10,000
  • Spread: bid aur ask ka farq — broker ki commission ka hissa
  • Leverage: chhote deposit se bara position kholne ki sahulat — leverage ki poori wazahat

Lot size ka sahi hisaab lagana position sizing ke through — humara position size calculator yeh kaam aasaan kar deta hai.

3. Forex Mein Munafa Aur Nuqsan Kaise Hota Hai?

Qeemat ki chali (movement) hi aapka P&L banati hai. 50 pip ka movement agar aapki direction mein ho to faida, khilaf ho to nuqsan.

ExampleHisaab
EUR/USD 1.0850 par buy1 lot, 50 pip faida = ~$50
Stop-loss 30 pip1 lot par ~$30 nuqsan
Ratio50:30 ≈ 1:1.6 (thoda theek hai, 1:2 behtar)

Leverage dono taraf kaam karta hai — 50x leverage par 2% chalat bhi aapka poora deposit kha sakti hai. Isi liye stop-loss aur chhota lot size zaroori hain. Forex mein sab se bara qatl kaaran bad lot size aur bina stop-loss trading hai.

4. Forex Aur Crypto Mein Kya Farq Hai?

Dono 24 ghante nahi chalte (forex 24/5, crypto 24/7) aur dono mein leverage milta hai. Asal farq underlying asset ka hai — forex mein mulki economies chalti hain, crypto mein supply-demand aur narrative.

  • Forex zyada predictable kabhi kabhi (news events, interest rates)
  • Crypto zyada volatile aur 24/7
  • Forex brokers leverage 50x-500x dete hain, crypto exchanges aksar 20x-125x
  • Shuru karne mein forex mein broker selection zyada ahem hai

Detail comparison: Forex vs Crypto 2026 guide.

5. Pakistan Se Forex Kaise Shuru Karein?

Forex trading karne ke liye aapko sirf internet aur ek broker account ki zaroorat hai:

  1. Broker chunein: verified regulation (FCA, ASIC, CySEC waghera) wale broker check karein, reviews parhein
  2. Account banayein: KYC pura karein — asal documents
  3. Deposit: chhoti rakam se shuru karein (bank transfer, P2P, cards — broker par depend)
  4. Demo account: pehle 1-2 mahine demo par practice — yeh step kabhi skip na karein
  5. Mini/micro lot: live mein 0.01 lot se shuru karein

Yaad rakhein: Pakistan mein forex regulation ka koi mukammal framework nahi — is liye broker ki regulation aur withdrawal history zaroor check karein. Pehla deposit hi woh rakam ho jise khone par aap ko farq na pade — capital guide parhein.

6. Forex Sessions Aur Waqt

24/5 ka matlab yeh nahi ke har waqt ek jaisa market ho. Teen ahem sessions:

  • Asian (Tokyo): thoda kam movement, USD/JPY active
  • London: sab se zyada volume — setups aksar yahin bante hain
  • New York: US news ke saath tez movement; London se overlap mein sab se zyada riqaaq

Pakistan ke waqt (PKT) ke mutabiq London session dopahar shuru hota hai — office workers ke liye sham ka waqt suitable ho sakta hai. Har session mein trade karna zaroori nahi — apna fixed waqt chunein, daily routine banayein.

7. Forex Ke Khatre Kya Hain?

Forex mein 70-80% retail traders nuqsan mein khatam hote hain — yeh broker reports khud karti hain. Asal wajuhat:

  • Zyada leverage: 100x+ par ek chhoti chalat sab kuch khatam
  • Bagair seekhe trading: demo chhod kar seedha live jana
  • News ke waqt trading: interest rate announcements par spread barh jata hai
  • Exotic pairs: USD/PKR jaise pairs mein spread itna zyada ke munafa mushkil

Forex scam se bachne ke liye: trading scams guide parhein. Aur risk ka sahi hisaab: Risk Management book.

Aap Ke Sawalat (FAQ)

Forex trading Pakistan mein legal hai?

Pakistan mein retail forex ka koi mukammal regulatory framework nahi hai — is liye zyada tar traders international regulated brokers se kaam lete hain. Broker ki regulation, reviews aur withdrawal history pehle verify karein.

Forex mein kitne paise se shuru ho sakta hai?

Broker account $10-50 se bhi khul jata hai, lekin itne chhote deposit par micro lot bhi mushkil se manage hota hai. Realistic shuru point — jahan 1% risk bhi kuch mayne rakhta ho — humari capital guide mein hai.

Forex aur crypto konsa behtar hai beginners ke liye?

Crypto aasaan access aur 24/7 market ke liye beginners ko attractive lagta hai; forex zyada structured lekin leverage aur broker selection ki wajah se risky hai. Dono mein pehle demo par practice zaroori hai.

Forex se full-time income ban sakti hai?

Shuru ke 6-12 mahine mein nahi. Jo log consistently risk manage karte hain unke liye possible hai, lekin pehle part-time rakhein aur realistic targets rakhein — 2-5% mahana ek sane target hai.
