Stock Market Kya Hai?

Stock market woh jagah hai jahan kompaniyon ke shares khareede aur beche jate hain. Jab aap kisi company ka share khareedte hain, aap us company ka chhota malik ban jate hain.

Is guide mein hum samjhenge ke stock market kaise kaam karta hai, shares se paise kaise bante hain, aur Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) se trading kaise shuru karein.

1. Shares Ka Matlab Kya Hai?

Company ko paise chahiye to woh apna kuch hissa chhota-chhota tukdon mein bech deti hai — yeh tukde hi shares (hissey) hain. Har share ki apni qeemat hoti hai jo market mein har lamha badalti rehti hai.

  • 100 shares khareedne ka matlab: company ka 100-piece mein se ek hissa
  • Qeemat barhe to aapka investment barha
  • Kuch shares market cap ke hisab se bade hote hain (blue chip), kuch chhote (small cap)
  • Mulk bhar ke sab se bari companies ka index: PSX-100, S&P 500

2. Stock Market Kaise Kaam Karta Hai?

Stock market ek auction jaisa hai: khareedne walon aur bechne walon ki qeemat milti hai to trade banti hai. Yeh supply aur demand se hi chalta hai.

  1. Company IPO ke zariye apne shares public ko deti hai (pehli baar bechna)
  2. Uske baad shares investors ke darmiyan trade hote hain
  3. Exchange (PSX, NYSE) har trade ko regulate aur record karta hai
  4. Broker ke zariye aap order lagate hain — seedha exchange se nahi

Jab kisi company ki kamai barhti hai ya umeed barhti hai, log zyada khareedte hain — qeemat barhti hai. Ulti khabar mein bechne walon ki bheer lagti hai — qeemat girti hai.

3. Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) Kya Hai?

PSX Pakistan ka stock exchange hai — Karachi mein. Yahan 500+ listed companies ke shares trade hote hain: banks, cement, pharma, fertilizer, energy sab shamil hain.

  • Trading ke waqt: hafta se juma, dopahar tak (PKT)
  • Minimum investment: kai brokers se chhote paise se bhi shuru — 1 share se
  • Account ke liye CNIC, bank account aur broker agreement zaroori
  • Dividend aur capital gains — stocks se do tarah ki kamai

PSX ki sab se bari khasoosiyat: lamba sabr yahan zyada kaam aata hai — arsaal purani kompaniyon ke shares mein riqaaq kam hota hai, is liye din ki trading (day trading) mushkil hoti hai, long-term investment aasaan.

4. Stock Market Se Paise Kaise Bante Hain?

Do tareeqe hain:

  • Price appreciation: share khareed kar rakhna, qeemat barhna, bechna — misal: 500 par khareeda, 650 par becha = 30% faida
  • Dividend: kuch kompaniyan har saal hisse-daron ko munafa baantti hain — rakh kar bhi kamai

Doosri taraf: qeemat girne par nuqsan bhi hota hai aur kabhi kabhi company khares (delist) bhi ho sakti hai. Isi liye diversification zaroori — ek hi company par sab mat lagayein. Index fund ya ETF ek aisa tareeqa hai jismein aap poori market ka hissa le lete hain.

6. Naye Investor Ke Liye Zaroori Baatein

  • Pehle seekhein: chart reading aur fundamental analysis ki bunyad
  • Diversify: ek sector ya ek company par sab na lagayein
  • Chhoti shuruaat: pehle wo paise lagayein jiska nuqsan bardasht ho sakay
  • Bechne ka plan: khareedne se pehle hi decide karein ke kis qeemat par nikalenge
  • Rozana charts par mat marte rahein: stocks long-term game hain — hafta mein ek baar kaafi

Stock market scam se bachne ke liye fraud guide parhein — "guaranteed returns" wale tips sab se bara khatra hain.

Aap Ke Sawalat (FAQ)

Stock market mein kitne paise se shuru ho sakta hai?

PSX ke brokers se aap 1 share se bhi khareed sakte hain — chand hazaar rupees se bhi shuru mumkin hai. Lekin itne chhote paise par diversification mushkil hota hai, is liye thoda sabr karke capital banayein.

Stock trading aur investing mein farq kya hai?

Investing mein aap saalon ke liye shares khareed kar rakhte hain aur dividend ka faida lete hain; trading mein hafton mein qeemat ke farq se munafa kamate hain. Naye logon ke liye investing zyada safe starting point hai.

PSX mein account kaise banwayein?

Kisi bhi SECP-registered broker ke zariye CNIC, bank account aur bank statement ke saath account khulta hai. Broker ki app se aap khareed-o-farokht kar sakte hain. Pehle broker ki fees aur reviews zaroor dekhein.

Stock market mein guaranteed returns milti hain?

Nahi. Koi bhi "guaranteed returns" wala dawa fraud hai. Market mein nuqsan ho sakta hai — jo log risk manage karte hain aur lamba sabr karte hain unhi ka track record acha rehta hai.
