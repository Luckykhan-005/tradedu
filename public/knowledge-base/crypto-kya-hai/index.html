Cryptocurrency Kya Hai?

Cryptocurrency ek digital currency hai jo internet par kaam karti hai aur jise koi bank ya hakoomat nahi chalati — balki code aur network chalata hai. Bitcoin sab se pehla aur sab se mashhoor crypto hai.

Is guide mein hum crypto ki bunyad, Bitcoin, wallets, exchanges aur yeh samjhenge ke Pakistan se crypto kaise khareeda ja sakta hai — bina technical jargon ke.

1. Crypto Aur Aam Currency Mein Kya Farq Hai?

Rs. aur Dollar ki qeemat hakoomat (State Bank) sambhalti hai aur woh chhapne aur print karne wali machine ke mutabiq chalti hai. Crypto ki qeemat supply aur demand se banti hai — koi central bank nahi.

  • Sab se baraa farq: crypto 24/7 khula hai, duniya kahin bhi, kisi bhi waqt
  • Har transaction public ledger (blockchain) par record hota hai
  • Paise bhejne ke liye bank ki ijazat nahi chahiye — sirf internet chahiye
  • Supply often fixed hai (Bitcoin: 21 million se zyada nahi honge)

Qeemat mein volatility bohat zyada hai — yeh crypto ki sab se bari khasoosiyat aur sab se bara khatra dono hai.

2. Bitcoin Kya Hai?

Bitcoin (BTC) 2009 mein bana — duniya ka pehla cryptocurrency. Iska banane wala "Satoshi Nakamoto" naam se jaana jata hai (asal shanakht kabhi nahi hui).

  • Maximum supply: 21 million BTC — isliye "digital sona" kaha jata hai
  • Har 4 saal mein mining reward aadha hota hai (halving) — supply dhimi hoti jaati hai
  • Ab tak ka sab se bara cryptocurrency — market ka aadha se zyada hissa
  • Ethereum (ETH) doosra bara coin hai — smart contracts ke liye mashhoor

Altcoin yaani Bitcoin ke ilawa baqi sab coins — altcoin ki wazahat glossary mein.

3. Blockchain Kya Hai? (Ek Line Mein)

Blockchain ek public register hai jismein har transaction likhi jati hai aur use koi mita nahi sakta. Har naya page (block) pichle page se juda hota hai — isliye "chain".

Yeh technology hi cryptocurrency ko khassa banati hai: beech mein koi bank nahi, lekin phir bhi kisi ke saath dhokha mumkin nahi kyunki record sab ke saamne khula hai. Aap ko blockchain developer nahi banna — sirf itna samajhna kaafi hai ke qeemat kis network ki demand par chalti hai.

4. Wallet Aur Exchange Kya Hai?

Yeh do alag cheezein hain jo aksar confuse ho jati hain:

ExchangeWallet
Kya haiTrading platform (Binance waghera)Coin rakhne ki jagah (app ya device)
UseKhareed-o-farokht, tradingLong-term holding, security
MatlabTijori ka counterAapki apni tijori
  • Hot wallet: internet se juda app — aasaan, thoda zyada khatra
  • Cold wallet: offline device — long-term holding ke liye behtar
  • Wallet aur exchange dono ki mukammal wazahat glossary mein hai

Coin ko exchange par hi saalon ke liye rakhna khatarnak hai — exchange hack ya band ho to paisa doob sakta hai.

5. Pakistan Se Crypto Kaise Khareedein?

Practical steps:

  1. Account banayein: Binance jaise bade exchange par signup + KYC pura karein
  2. Paise deposit karein: P2P trading se bank transfer ya local methods — Binance P2P guide mukammal parhein
  3. Chhoti miqdar se shuru: pehle sikhs — $10-20 bhi kaafi hai practice ke liye
  4. Security on karein: 2FA zaroor lagayein, seed phrase kisi ko na batayein
  5. Long-term ho to wallet mein le jayein: exchange se personal wallet mein withdraw

Crypto kis tarike se trading ban jati hai — trading ki bunyad parhein, aur crypto futures ke liye Crypto Futures book.

6. Crypto Ke Khatre Aur Scams

Crypto mein itna faida hai jitna khatra — dono sath chalte hain:

  • Volatility: ek haftay mein 20-30% chalat aam hai — volatility
  • Rug pulls: naye coins ki team paisa le kar bhaag jati hai — rug pull
  • Fake signals/gurus: "guaranteed profit" dene wale sab fraud — scams se bachein
  • Leverage: futures mein 100x = chhoti chalat mein liquidation — liquidation

Qawaid: jo cheez aap ko samajh nahi aati, us mein paise mat lagayein. Pehle parhein, phir demo/testnet par practice karein, phir chhota live.

7. Crypto Long-Term Investment Hai Ya Trading?

Dono tareeqe mawjood hain:

  • HODL: achay coins khareed kar saalon ke liye rakhna — HODL ka matlab
  • Trading: hafte/din ke setups se chhoti munafa kamana — Moving Averages book parhein

Naye logon ke liye behtar tareeqa: 80% portfolio long-term holdings, 20% se trading practice. Har cheez ko leverage par khelna sab se tezi se nuqsan ka raasta hai.

Aap Ke Sawalat (FAQ)

Crypto Pakistan mein legal hai?

Pakistan mein crypto ki legal status abhi tay nahi hai — SBP aur regulator waqt ke sath rules badalte hain. Is liye current halaat waqt khud verify karein aur sirf woh paise lagayein jiska nuqsan bardasht ho sakay.

Bitcoin ka future kya hai?

Koi nahi jaanta — yeh kisi ko bhi pata ho to woh jhoot bol raha hai. Bitcoin ki limited supply aur badti adoption isko strong banati hai, lekin qeemat gir bhi sakti hai. Decision hamesha khud research (DYOR) ke baad lein.

Kitne paise se crypto shuru karein?

Technical tor par $10 se bhi. Lekin chhote paise par fees proportionally zyada lagti hain. $50-100 practice ke liye kaafi hain — aur sirf utna hi lagayein jiska poora nuqsan ho sakay.

Crypto wallet safe kaise rakhein?

2FA on rakhein, seed phrase ko kisi ko na batayein (na email, na WhatsApp), long-term holdings cold wallet mein rakhein aur exchange par sirf trading amount rakhein. Public WiFi par trading se bachein.
