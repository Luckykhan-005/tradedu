Trading Kaise Shuru Karein? — 7 Practical Steps

Naya trader banne ka sawal aksar yeh hota hai: "shuru kahan se karun?" Zyada tar log seedha account bana kar paise lagate hain aur nuqsan mein khatam karte hain. Sahi tareeqa qadam dar qadam hai.

Yeh guide 7 practical steps mein hai — har step par kitna waqt lagta hai aur kya karna hai. Isay follow karein to aap bina jaldi kiye trading seekh sakte hain.

Step 1: Trading ki Bunyad Parhein (2-3 Hafte)

Pehle mahina sirf parhne aur samajhne ke liye — paise lagane ke liye nahi. Aap ko yeh samajhna hai:

  • Market kaise kaam karti hai — orders, bid/ask, spread
  • Candlesticks kaise padhte hain — chart reading guide
  • Support, resistance aur trend kya hai
  • Risk management ke qawaid — 1% rule, stop-loss, risk-reward

TradeEd par 30+ free books aur 75 terms wala glossary hain — is step ke liye kaafi hain.

Step 2: Apna Market Choose Karein

Ek market choose karein aur usi mein mahir hone ki koshish karein — shuru mein teenon ek saath nahi.

Step 3: Account Banayein (1 Din)

Broker ya exchange par account banayein. Chhote tips:

  • Sirf verified platforms use karein (Binance jaise exchanges, PSX ke registered brokers)
  • KYC pura karein — asal naam, asal documents
  • 2-factor authentication (2FA) abhi on karein
  • Account mein paise dalne se pehle demo/test trading ka option dhoondein
  • Agar forex broker chun rahe hain to regulation check karein

Binance par account aur P2P se paise dalne ka complete tareeqa: Binance P2P guide (Urdu).

Step 4: Demo Trading Karein (1-2 Mahine)

Yeh sab se ahem step hai aur 90% log isay chhod dete hain. Demo account par fake paise se asli market ki practice karein.

  • Hafte mein 4-5 din, roz kam az kam 30 minute trading karein
  • Har trade apne journal mein likhein (snapshot + kyun liya)
  • Apni 5-10 trades ki series banayein — strategy test karein
  • Jab tak consistently 2 mahine profit na aaye, live mat jayein

Demo par mirchi nahi lagti — is liye yahan aap apni asal jazbaati galtiyan pakad sakte hain: revenge trading, FOMO, overtrading.

Step 5: Apni Strategy Likhein

Strategy likhna matlab apne trading ke qawaid kaghaz par likhna. Ek simple strategy mein hota hai:

  1. Entry: kis condition mein trade lenge (misal: support par bullish engulfing + volume)
  2. Stop-loss: kahan nuqsan rokna hai
  3. Target: kitne faide par nikalna hai (kam az kam 1:2 ratio)
  4. Position size: kitna lot/volume — position size calculator use karein
  5. Waqt: kis timeframe par, kis session mein trade karna hai

Likhi hui strategy par amal karna — aur use backtesting se test karna — aapko disciplined banata hai.

Step 6: Journal Banayein Aur Seekhein

Har trade log karein: entry, exit, screenshot, kyun liya, kya galti hui, kaisa mehsoos hua. Mahine ke aakhir mein total dekhein.

  • Kitne trades jeete, kitne hare
  • Average munafa vs average nuqsan
  • Kis waqt sab se zyada galtiyan hoti hain
  • Konsa setup sab se acha chala

Yeh data hi batata hai ke aap ki trading improve ho rahi hai ya nahi. Journal banane ki poori tafseel: trading journal kyun zaroori hai. TradeEd par free trading journal tool bhi hai.

Step 7: Chhote Capital Se Live Trading

Jab aapke paas likhi hui strategy, 2 mahine ka demo record aur journal ho — tab live trading shuru karein, lekin bilkul chhoti miqdar se.

  • 1% risk rule — har trade par qatal capital se zyada na risk karein
  • Capital itna rakhein ke 1% bhi meaningful ho — kitna paisa lagana chahiye
  • Pehle 1-2 mahine ka target sirf seekhna hai, kamana nahi
  • Live trading mein galti ho to wapas demo par jaana sharm nahi

Aap Ke Sawalat (FAQ)

Trading seekhne mein kitna waqt lagta hai?

Basic samajhne mein 2-4 hafte, consistent banne mein 6-12 mahine. Har koi alag hai — lekin jo log demo aur journal nahi chhodte unke aage badhne ke zyada chances hain.

Shuru ke liye konsa market behtar hai?

Crypto (24/7, chhote paise) ya stocks (PSX/US) beginners ke liye aasaan hain. Forex leverage ki wajah se seekhne mein thoda mushkil hai. Pehle ek market choose karein aur usi par focus rakhein.

Kya trading ke liye course zaroori hai?

Zaroori nahi — free books aur guides kaafi hain bunyad ke liye. Course tab useful jab aap structured practice chahte hain. Bina kisi bhi source ke sirf YouTube shorts se seekhna kaafi nahi hota.

Kya main job ke saath trading kar sakta hoon?

Haan, aur aksar yahi behtar hai. Swing trading aur daily 30-60 minute ka routine working log ke liye suitable hai. Rozana full-time jaisi trading job chhorne ki zaroorat shuru mein bilkul nahi.
