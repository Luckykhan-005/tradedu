1. Introduction – Order Types kyun zaroori hain?

Futures trading mein order type aapka sab se powerful tool hai. Galat order type choose karne se slippage, missed fill, ya badi loss ho sakti hai. Professional traders order type ko strategy ke hisse ke tor par treat karte hain, sirf “button” nahi.

Core Principle: Har order type ek trade-off hai — Fill Certainty vs Price Certainty. Market order fill guarantee karta hai lekin price nahi. Limit order price guarantee karta hai lekin fill nahi.

Is guide mein hum Binance, Bybit, OKX, CME jaise platforms par available major order types cover karenge. Crypto perpetual futures aur traditional futures (ES, NQ, CL) dono ke liye concepts same hain.

Order Types ka Overview

Order Type Fill Guarantee Price Control Best Use
Market Haan Nahi Emergency exit / Fast entry
Limit Nahi Haan Planned entry / Take-profit
Stop Market Haan (trigger ke baad) Nahi Stop-loss (protective)
Stop Limit Nahi Haan Controlled exit (stable market)
Trailing Stop Haan (usually) Dynamic Trend following / Profit lock
Post Only Nahi (reject ho sakta) Haan Maker fee save
Scaled Partial Haan Large size without market impact

2. Market Order – Speed ka Badshah

Kya hai Market Order?

Market order exchange ko bolta hai: “Abhi fill karo, best available price pe.” Aap speed choose karte ho, price control nahi.

Market buy order instantly best ask se match hota hai. Agar size bari ho to multiple levels se fill hota hai → average price worse ho sakta hai.

Kab Use Karein?

  • Best Losing position ko turant band karna (stop-loss trigger ke baad)
  • Best Strong breakout / momentum trade jahan miss karna costly ho
  • Best High liquidity markets (BTCUSDT, ES, NQ) normal conditions mein

Kab Avoid Karein?

  • Avoid Thin / low liquidity coins ya after-hours sessions
  • Avoid Major news events (FOMC, NFP, CPI) ke around jab spreads widen hote hain
  • Avoid Jab 1-2 ticks slippage aapka R:R ratio kharab kar de
Example (BTCUSDT Perpetual):
Current price: 64,250. Aap long position close karna chahte ho emergency mein. Market sell order place kiya → fill 64,248 pe (2$ slippage). Liquid market mein ye normal hai. Agar aap limit 64,250 pe lagate to shayad fill na hota aur loss badh jata.
Pro Tip: Market order kabhi bhi “default” entry ke liye mat use karein. Entry ke liye limit prefer karein; exit (especially stop-loss) ke liye market better hai.

3. Limit Order – Price Control ka Master

Kya hai Limit Order?

Limit order aapko price specify karne deta hai. Buy limit sirf aapke price ya usse better (lower) pe fill hoga. Sell limit sirf aapke price ya usse better (higher) pe.

Limit order order book mein rest karta hai. Fill tabhi hota hai jab market aapke price tak aaye.

Kab Use Karein?

  • Support / Resistance / VWAP pe planned entry
  • Take-profit target set karna
  • Range-bound / calm markets jahan patience reward hoti hai
  • Maker fee save karna (Post Only ke sath)

Kab Avoid Karein?

  • Fast breakout jahan miss karna costly ho
  • Emergency exit (stop-loss)
  • Highly volatile news periods
Example (ES Futures):
ES 5205 pe trade kar raha hai. Aap support 5200 pe buy karna chahte ho. Buy Limit @ 5200 lagaya. Agar price 5200 touch kare to fill, warna order open rehta hai. Market order se aap 5205 pe enter karte – 5 points zyada.
Professional Habit: Entry ke liye Limit, Exit (protective stop) ke liye Stop-Market. Ye combination most consistent results deta hai.

4. Conditional Orders – Stop Market & Stop Limit

Conditional order (ya Stop order) dormant rehta hai jab tak trigger price touch na ho. Trigger hone ke baad ye Market ya Limit order ban jata hai.

4.1 Stop Market Order

Trigger price touch hote hi Market order ban jata hai. Fill guarantee, price nahi.

Price Trigger
Touch
→
Order Activate
(Market)
→
Instant Fill
(Slippage possible)

Kab Use Karein?

  • Protective stop-loss (long ke neeche / short ke upar)
  • Breakout entry (buy stop above resistance)
  • Volatile markets / news events – jahan exit zaroori ho
Critical Warning: Stop-Limit ko protective stop ke liye mat use karein agar aap gap risk tolerate nahi kar sakte. Gap through limit hone pe aapka stop fail ho jata hai aur position open reh jati hai.

4.2 Stop Limit Order

Trigger ke baad Limit order ban jata hai. Price control hai, lekin fill guarantee nahi.

Factor Stop Market Stop Limit
Execution Guarantee Haan Nahi (gap risk)
Price Certainty Nahi Haan
Best Market Volatile / News Range-bound / Liquid
Risk Slippage Missed fill
Practical Setup (Long BTC):
Entry: 64,000
Stop Market: 63,200 (risk ~800$)
Agar aap Stop Limit use karte ho (Trigger 63,200, Limit 63,100) to flash crash mein price 63,000 pe jump kar sakta hai aur aapka order unfilled reh jata hai.

Conditional Order on Binance / Bybit

Modern platforms pe Conditional order mein aap choose kar sakte ho:

  • Trigger: Mark Price ya Last Price
  • Order type after trigger: Limit / Market / BBO

Recommendation: Stop-loss ke liye Mark Price trigger prefer karein (manipulation se bachne ke liye).

5. Trailing Stop Order – Profit Lock karne ka Tarika

Kya hai Trailing Stop?

Trailing stop dynamic stop-loss hai jo price ke sath favorable direction mein move karta hai, lekin kabhi unfavorable direction mein nahi. Aap ek distance (points / % / ticks) set karte ho.

Long position: Price up jate hi stop upar move karta hai. Price reverse hote hi stop fixed rehta hai aur trigger hota hai.

Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? (Long Example)

  1. Entry @ 60,000 + 3% trailing stop → Initial stop = 58,200
  2. Price 62,000 pe jata hai → Stop moves to 60,140
  3. Price 65,000 pe jata hai → Stop moves to 63,050
  4. Price reverse hoke 63,050 touch karta hai → Market sell trigger → Profit lock

Kab Use Karein?

  • Strong trending market mein profit maximize karna
  • Jab aapko exact exit level nahi pata (trend following)
  • Partial profit leke remaining position ko trail karna

Kab Avoid Karein?

  • Choppy / ranging market – whipsaw se bar bar stop hit hota hai
  • Bahut tight trail (normal volatility se kam) – premature exit
Pro Tip: Trail distance ko ATR (Average True Range) se set karein. Example: 1.5× ATR trail. Ye market ke natural noise se bachata hai.

6. Post Only Order – Maker Fee ka Fayda

Kya hai Post Only?

Post Only ek flag hai jo ensure karta hai ke aapka limit order sirf Maker ban ke order book mein add ho. Agar order turant match hota (Taker ban jata) to system usse reject kar deta hai.

Kab Use Karein?

  • High-frequency ya high-volume traders jo fee edge chahte hain
  • Calm markets jahan aap wait kar sakte ho
  • Market making / passive strategies

Kab Avoid Karein?

  • Urgent entry / exit
  • Fast markets – rejection se trade miss ho sakta hai
  • Jab fill zaroori ho aur fee secondary ho
Fee Impact Example (Binance Futures VIP0):
Maker: ~0.02% | Taker: ~0.05%
10 BTC @ 60,000 = 600,000 USDT notional
Maker fee: 120 USDT | Taker fee: 300 USDT
Difference: 180 USDT per trade. Daily 5 trades pe ~900 USDT save possible.

7. Scaled Order – Badi Size ko Smart Split

Kya hai Scaled Order?

Scaled order ek badi quantity ko multiple chhote limit orders mein split karta hai, ek price range ke andar. Har sub-order alag price pe place hota hai taake market impact kam ho.

Scaled order price range mein multiple limit orders generate karta hai. Badi size ko chhupa ke market impact kam karta hai.

Parameters jo Set Karte Hain

  • Min Price / Max Price – Range define
  • Number of Orders – Kitne sub-orders
  • Total Size – Overall quantity
  • Allocation Mode – Flat (equal), Increasing (neeche zyada), Decreasing

Kab Use Karein?

  • Large position build karna without moving market
  • Accumulation / distribution strategies
  • Jab aap average entry improve karna chahte ho
Pro Use Case: Institutional-style accumulation. 50 BTC long accumulate karna hai support zone mein. Scaled order 5–10 levels pe split karke quietly position build karein.

9. Real Trading Workflows & Examples

Workflow 1: Classic Long Setup (Day Trade)

Setup: BTC support 63,800 pe bounce expect
  1. Entry: Buy Limit @ 63,850 (thoda upar support se)
  2. Stop Loss: Stop Market @ 63,500 (risk defined)
  3. Take Profit 1: Sell Limit @ 64,400 (50% size)
  4. Remaining: Trailing Stop 1.5% se trail
Ye structure risk define karta hai aur profit maximize karta hai.

Workflow 2: Breakout Entry

Setup: Resistance 65,000 break
  1. Entry: Buy Stop Market @ 65,050 (confirmation ke baad)
  2. Stop: Stop Market @ 64,700
  3. Target: Limit @ 66,200 + Trailing on remaining

Workflow 3: Large Accumulation (Scaled)

Goal: 20 BTC accumulate 62,000–63,500 range mein
  1. Scaled Buy: Min 62,000 / Max 63,500 / 10 orders / Increasing allocation
  2. Har fill pe corresponding Stop Market lagana (ya average entry pe overall stop)
  3. Position complete hone pe Trailing Stop activate

10. Risk Management Best Practices

Golden Rule: Har open position ke sath active protective stop hona chahiye. “Mental stop” professional trading mein acceptable nahi.
  • Position Size pehle decide karein – Risk % account se calculate karke, phir order type choose karein.
  • Stop-loss ke liye Stop Market prefer karein – especially volatile sessions mein.
  • Slippage ko risk calculation mein include karein – especially thin markets ya news ke around.
  • Post Only sirf tab jab aap fill miss karne ka risk le sakte ho.
  • Trailing Stop ko ATR based rakhein taake noise se bach sakein.
  • One trade = One plan – Entry, Stop, Target pehle se decide, order types us hisab se.
Common Mistake: Beginners aksar Stop Limit protective stop ke liye use karte hain “price control” ke liye. Reality: gap through limit = unprotected position. Professional consensus – protective stops ke liye Stop Market better hai.

12. Final Summary & Pro Tips

Key Takeaways

  • Market = Speed. Emergency aur momentum ke liye.
  • Limit = Price control. Planned entries aur targets ke liye.
  • Stop Market = Protective stop ka gold standard.
  • Stop Limit = Controlled exit, lekin gap risk.
  • Trailing Stop = Trend ka saath dena + profit lock.
  • Post Only = Fee edge, passive strategies.
  • Scaled = Large size, low footprint.
Professional Mantra:
“Entry pe price control lo (Limit), Exit pe fill control lo (Stop Market / Market). Trend mein trail karo. Size bari ho to scale karo. Fee matter kare to Post Only.”
