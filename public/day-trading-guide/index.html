Day Trading Kya Hai?

Day trading ka matlab hai kisi bhi financial instrument ko usi din khareedna aur bechna — rat ko apne saath position le kar nahi jana.

Day Trading ki Definition

Day trading ek trading style hai jismein trader apni positions usi din open karta hai aur usi din close karta hai. Market band hone se pehle tamam trades band ho jaati hain. Ye stocks, forex, commodities, aur crypto sab mein ho sakta hai.

Day traders ka maqsad chote-chote price movements se profit kamana hota hai. Wo din mein kai baar trade karte hain — kabhi 2-3, kabhi 10-20 trades bhi. Har trade se thoda profit uthate hain aur din ke akhir mein unki positions zero hoti hain.

DAY TRADING KA FUNDAMENTAL RULE
Subah trade kholein → Din bhar monitor karein → Shaam tak close karein Koi bhi position overnight nahi jaati. Ye risk ko kam karta hai.

Trading Styles ki Comparison

Ye samajhna zaroori hai k day trading baqi trading styles se kaise alag hai. Har style ka apna time horizon hai aur apna risk profile hai.

Day Trading ke Pros aur Cons

✓ FAAYDE

  • Koi overnight risk nahi — tamam trades usi din band ho jaati hain.
  • Quick results — aap ko pata chal jata hai k trade mein profit hua ya loss.
  • Compounding — daily profit reinvest kar ke tezi se barha sakte hain.
  • Market hours mein flexibility — aap apni marzi se trading kar sakte hain.
  • Kam capital se shuru — forex aur crypto mein choti rakaam se bhi shuru kar sakte hain.

✗ NUQSAN

  • Zyada stress — constantly screen par rehna hota hai.
  • Commission/fees — zyada trades = zyada fees.
  • Overtrading ka khatra — jaldi jaldi mein galat trades le lena.
  • Emotional trading — loss mein revenge trading karna.
  • Time consuming — poora din research aur monitoring chahiye.
! Warning: Day trading mein 90% log initial stages mein paisa gawa dete hain. Sirf wahi log consistently kamate hain jo proper training, risk management, aur discipline ke saath trading karte hain. Bina seekhe seedha live market mein mat jaayein.
PRACTICE QUESTIONS

Q1: Day trading mein position kitne tak hold ki jaati hai?

Day trading ka rule ye hai k trader apni tamam positions usi din close karta hai. Koi bhi position overnight nahi hoti.

Q2: Day trading ka sab se bada khatra kya hai?

Overtrading aur emotional decisions day trading ka sab se bada khatra hai. Bina strategy ke jaldi jaldi trades lena nuqsan ka sab se bara sabab hai.

Day Trading Ka Setup

Sahi setup hona day trading ki safalta ka pehla qadam hai. Platform, charts, indicators, aur screen layout sab kuch hona chahiye.

Platform Choose Karein

Day trading ke liye aap ko ek reliable trading platform chahiye jo fast execution de aur kam fees ho. Crypto ke liye Binance aur Bybit sab se popular hain.

  • Binance — sab se bara crypto exchange, kam fees (0.1%), futures bhi available hain.
  • Bybit — futures ke liye best, user-friendly interface.
  • TradingView — charting ke liye sab se accha tool, free aur paid dono versions hain.

Chart Setup

Day trading ke liye aap ko multiple timeframes dekhne chahiye. Aik chart par 1-2 timeframe kaafi nahi hote. Kam se kam 2-3 timeframes par nazar rakhna chahiye.

RECOMMENDED TIMEFRAMES
Higher TF (1hr/4hr) → Trend dekhein
Middle TF (15min/30min) → Structure samjhein
Entry TF (1min/5min) → Entry/Exit lein Higher timeframe ka trend hamesha respect karein. Chota timeframe sirf entry ke liye hai.

Recommended Indicators

Har indicator ka apna kaam hai. Day trading ke liye ye indicators sab se zyada useful hain:

  • EMA (9, 21, 50) — trend direction aur dynamic support/resistance.
  • VWAP — average price ka benchmark, institutional traders bhi use karte hain.
  • RSI (14) — overbought/oversold zones identify karna.
  • Volume Profile — kahan sab se zyada trading hui hai.

Market Sessions & Timing

Crypto 24/7 Market

Forex market mein 3 major trading sessions hain. Har session ka apna character hai:

  • Asian Session (Tokyo) — Subah 6:00 AM se 3:00 PM PKT. Kam volatility, chote moves.
  • London Session — Dophar 1:00 PM se 10:00 PM PKT. High volume, strong trends.
  • New York Session — Raat 6:00 PM se 3:00 AM PKT. Sab se zyada volatility, major news releases.

Support & Resistance (Intraday)

Support aur Resistance level identify karna day trading ki sab se bunyadi skill hai. Ye levels batate hain k price rukegi kahan ya reverse hogi kahan.

Support aur Resistance kya hai?

Support woh price level hai jahan buyers active ho jaate hain aur price neeche nahi jaati. Jaise koi floor ho — price girti hai toh yahan ruk jaati hai.

Resistance woh price level hai jahan sellers active ho jaate hain aur price upar nahi jaati. Jaise koi ceiling ho — price upar jaati hai toh yahan se reject ho jaati hai.

S/R LEVELS KA BASIC RULE
Price = Support → BUY zone
Price = Resistance → SELL zone
Level tod diya → Role reverse hota hai Purana resistance naya support ban jata hai, aur pura support naya resistance. Ye "Role Reversal" kahlata hai.

S/R Levels kaise draw karein?

Chart par S/R levels draw karna ek art hai. Ye steps follow karein:

  1. Chart par wo jagah dhundhein jahan price ne 2 ya us se zyada baar reverse kiya ho.
  2. Prior swing highs aur lows dekhein — ye natural resistance/support hain.
  3. Round numbers dekhein — jaise 40000, 42500, 45000. Psychological levels hote hain.
  4. Volume clusters dekhein — jahan sab se zyada volume thi, wahan strong level hota hai.

Round Numbers & Pivot Points

Round numbers (jaise 40000, 50000, 100000) psychologically important hote hain. Logon ka dil in numbers par buy/sell karne ka hota hai. Pivot points automatic calculate hone wale levels hain jo previous day ke high, low, aur close se nikalte hain.

Intraday S/R Tips

  • Pehle 30 minute ka high aur low — ye aaj ke pehle S/R levels hain.
  • Previous day ka high, low, aur close — ye powerful levels hain.
  • Volume Profile se dekhein k sab se zyada trading kahan hui hai.
  • Hamesha multiple confirmations lein — sirf ek candle se decision na lein.
PRACTICE QUESTIONS

Q1: Agar price resistance level tod de toh kya hota hai?

Ye "Role Reversal" hai. Jab resistance tod diya jaata hai, toh wo level support ban jaata hai. Isi tarah support todne par wo resistance ban jaata hai.

Q2: Round numbers kyun important hain?

Round numbers (40000, 50000, 100000) psychological levels hain. Logon ka dil in numbers par buy/sell karne ka hota hai, is liye ye naturally strong S/R levels ban jaate hain.

Candlestick Patterns for Quick Trades

Candlestick patterns padhna day trading ki sab se zaroori skill hai. Ye patterns batate hain k market kis direction mein ja raha hai aur entry/exit kahan lena hai.

Top 5 Patterns jo Har Trader ko Aanay Chahiye

1. Engulfing Pattern

Engulfing tab banta hai jab ek choti candle ko agli bari candle puri tarah cover kar le. Ye reversal signal hai — agar uptrend mein bane toh bearish, agar downtrend mein bane toh bullish.

  • Bullish Engulfing: Downtrend ke baad green candle peechli red candle ko cover kare → BUY signal.
  • Bearish Engulfing: Uptrend ke baad red candle peechli green candle ko cover kare → SELL signal.

2. Pin Bar (Hammer / Shooting Star)

Pin bar mein ek lambi "tail" (wick) hoti hai aur choti body. Tail batati hai k price ek taraf gayi thi lekin wapas aa gayi.

  • Hammer (bullish): Uptrend ke baad, neeche lambi tail → buyers ne control le liya.
  • Shooting Star (bearish): Uptrend ke baad, upar lambi tail → sellers ne control le liya.

3. Doji

Doji mein open aur close almost same hota hai. Ye batata hai k buyers aur sellers mein tay hue hain. Trend reversal ka signal ho sakta hai.

4. Morning Star / Evening Star

Ye teen candle ka pattern hai. Morning Star bullish reversal hai, Evening Star bearish reversal hai.

5. Hammer (Alag se Important)

Hammer candle bottom par banti hai aur batati hai k sellers ne price neeche dhakela lekin buyers ne wapas upar khaincha. Ye strong reversal signal hai.

Bullish Engulfing tab banta hai jab downtrend mein ek bari green candle peechli choti red candle ko puri tarah cover kar le. Ye reversal signal hai.

Q2: Doji candle kya batati hai?

Doji mein open aur close almost same hota hai. Ye batata hai k buyers aur sellers mein tay hue hain — market indecision mein hai. Trend reversal ka signal ho sakta hai.

Volume & Order Flow

Volume batata hai k market mein kitni activity ho rahi hai. Bina volume ke sirf price dekhna adhoora hai. Volume se pata chalta hai k move asli hai ya fake.

Volume kyun Zaroori Hai?

Price ke saath volume dekhna bohot zaroori hai. Agar price barh rahi hai aur volume bhi barh raha hai — toh move strong hai. Agar price barh rahi hai lekin kam ho raha hai — toh move weak hai aur reversal ho sakta hai.

VOLUME + PRICE RULES
Price ↑ + Volume ↑ = STRONG UPTREND (Buy)
Price ↑ + Volume ↓ = WEAK MOVE (Caution)
Price ↓ + Volume ↑ = STRONG DOWNTREND (Sell)
Price ↓ + Volume ↓ = WEAK DROP (Possible reversal) Volume confirmation sab se powerful signal hai. Price bina volume ke jhooth bol sakta hai.

Volume Spikes kya hote hain?

Volume spike tab hota hai jab achanak bohot zyada buying ya selling hoti hai. Ye batata hai k kuch bada ho raha hai — koi bada player (institutional trader) enter ya exit kar raha hai.

  • Uptrend mein volume spike: Naye buyers aa rahe hain, trend strong ho raha hai.
  • Resistance par volume spike + rejection: Sellers ne entry li, trend reverse ho sakta hai.
  • Support par volume spike + bounce: Buyers ne entry li, support strong hai.
  • Breakout ke saath volume spike: Breakout genuine hai, chase kar sakte hain.

VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price)

VWAP ek powerful indicator hai jo batata hai k din mein average mein kahan trading ho rahi hai. Ye institutional traders ka benchmark hai.

  • Price VWAP ke upar hai → Bulls control mein hain.
  • Price VWAP ke neeche hai → Bears control mein hain.
  • VWAP ko support/resistance ki tarah treat kar sakte hain.
  • VWAP ke paas entry lena zyada safe hota hai.

Volume se Fake Moves Pakdein

REAL MOVE vs FAKE MOVE
Price barh rahi hai, volume barh raha haiREAL — Trend follow karein
Price barh rahi hai, volume kam ho raha haiFAKE — Reversal ka intezaar karein
Breakout hua, volume spike aayaGENUINE — Entry le sakte hain
Breakout hua, volume kam haiFAKE BREAKOUT — Door rahein
Price VWAP ke upar hai + volume strongBULLISH CONFIRMED
› Pro Tip: VWAP ke paas entry lena sab se safe hota hai. Jab price VWAP ko touch kare aur bounce kare, tab entry lein. Stop-loss VWAP ke neeche lagayein. Risk:Reward 1:2 ya us se zyada hona chahiye.
PRACTICE QUESTIONS

Q1: Price barh rahi hai lekin volume kam ho raha hai — iska kya matlab hai?

Jab price barh rahi hai lekin volume kam ho raha hai, toh move weak hai. Ye batata hai k naye buyers kam aa rahe hain aur trend reverse ho sakta hai.

Q2: VWAP ke upar price hone ka kya matlab hai?

Jab price VWAP ke upar hoti hai, iska matlab hai k buyers (bulls) control mein hain. VWAP ke neeche hone par sellers (bears) control mein hote hain.

Strategy — Trend Following

Trend following ka matlab hai k price jis direction mein ja rahi hai usi direction mein trade karna. "Trend is your friend" — ye day trading ki sab se purani aur sab se effective strategy hai.

Moving Average Crossover Strategy (EMA 9/21)

EMA (Exponential Moving Average) crossover strategy mein do EMAs use hote hain — EMA 9 (fast) aur EMA 21 (slow). Jab fast EMA upar se slow EMA ko cross kare (bullish crossover), toh buy signal milta hai. Jab neeche se cross kare (bearish crossover), toh sell signal milta hai.

EMA CROSSOVER RULES
BUY: EMA 9 > EMA 21 (Golden Cross) → Long Entry
SELL: EMA 9 < EMA 21 (Death Cross) → Short Entry Hamesha higher timeframe (15min/1hr) se trend confirm karein. Chote timeframe par whipsaws zyada hote hain.

Breakout Strategy (Support/Resistance Break)

Breakout strategy mein price ke kisi strong resistance level ko todne ka wait kiya jaata hai. Jab price resistance ke upar close ho aur volume barh jaye, toh ye genuine breakout hai aur long entry le sakte hain.

BREAKOUT ENTRY RULES
1. Resistance level identify karein (2+ touches)
2. Price resistance ke upar close ho
3. Volume spike ho (confirmation)
4. Breakout candle ke low par stop-loss lagayein Fake breakouts se bachne ke liye hamesha volume confirmation dekhein. Bina volume ke breakout ka matlab nahi.

Pullback Entries in Trending Market

Trend following mein sab se acchi entry tab hoti hai jab price pullback kare trend ke against. Agar uptrend hai toh price neeche aaye aur support ya EMA ko touch kare — yahan buy entry lein.

  • Uptrend: Price pullback karke EMA 21 ya previous resistance (ab support) ko touch kare → BUY
  • Downtrend: Price pullback karke EMA 21 ya previous support (ab resistance) ko touch kare → SELL
  • Confirmation: Pullback ke baad bullish/bearish candle baney

Entry Rules

  1. Pehle higher timeframe (15min/1hr) par trend identify karein.
  2. Entry timeframe (1min/5min) par EMA crossover ya breakout ka wait karein.
  3. Volume se confirmation lein.
  4. Candlestick pattern se additional confirmation (bullish engulfing, hammer).

Exit Rules

  • Take Profit: Risk:Reward 1:2 ya us se zyada hona chahiye.
  • Stop Loss: Entry ke neeche (long) ya upar (short) lagayein.
  • Trailing Stop: Jab price profit mein aaye, stop loss ko move karein.

Stop Loss Placement

STOP LOSS PLACEMENT OPTIONS
Below EMA 21Dynamic — trend ke saath move hota hai
Below recent swing lowStructure-based — zyada reliable
Fixed percentage (1-2%)Simple — lekin flexible nahi
ATR-based (1.5x ATR)Volatility-adjusted — best approach
PRACTICE QUESTIONS

Q1: EMA 9 jab EMA 21 ko upar se cross kare toh kya signal milta hai?

Jab fast EMA (9) slow EMA (21) ko upar se cross kare, ye Golden Cross kehlata hai aur ye buy signal hai.

Q2: Trend following mein entry ke liye sab se accha mauka kab hai?

Pullback mein entry lena sab se safe hota hai kyunki aap better price par entry paate hain aur risk kam hota hai.

Strategy — Reversal Trading

Reversal trading mein hum wo mauke dhoondhte hain jab market apni direction change karne wala ho. Ye trend following se alag hai — yahan hum against the trend jaate hain lekin confirmation ke saath.

RSI Divergence (Bullish/Bearish)

RSI divergence tab hota hai jab price aur RSI alag direction mein ja rahe hain. Ye reversal ka strong signal hai.

  • Bullish Divergence: Price lower low bana raha hai lekin RSI higher low bana raha hai → Upward reversal ka signal
  • Bearish Divergence: Price higher high bana raha hai lekin RSI lower high bana raha hai → Downward reversal ka signal
RSI DIVERGENCE RULES
1. RSI (14) use karein
2. Price ka swing low/high note karein
3. RSI ka swing low/high note karein
4. Agar direction alag hai = DIVERGENCE
5. Confirmation candle ka wait karein RSI divergence 100% accurate nahi hai. Hamesha volume aur candlestick confirmation dekhein.

Double Top aur Double Bottom

Ye classic reversal patterns hain jo chart par baar baar bante hain.

  • Double Top (M Pattern): Price do baar same resistance level ko touch kare aur neeche aaye → Bearish reversal
  • Double Bottom (W Pattern): Price do baar same support level ko touch kare aur upar jaaye → Bullish reversal

Support/Resistance Bounce Trades

Jab price kisi strong support ya resistance level par pohanchay aur wahan se reverse ho, toh ye bounce trade hai. Key levels hain:

  1. Previous day high/low
  2. Round numbers (40000, 42500, 45000)
  3. Volume profile ke high volume nodes
  4. Fibonacci retracement levels (38.2%, 50%, 61.8%)

Kab Reversals Trade NA Karein

! Warning: Reversal trading risky hai kyunki aap trend ke against ja rahe hain. In halaat mein reversal trade mat lein: (1) Jab market strong trend mein ho, (2) Jab major news release aane wali ho, (3) Jab low volume ho, (4) Jab bina confirmation ke sirf "lagta hai" ke basis par trade lein.
PRACTICE QUESTIONS

Q1: Bullish RSI Divergence mein kya hota hai?

Bullish divergence mein price toh neeche ja raha hota hai lekin RSI ke indicator mein opposite direction mein move hota hai — ye upward reversal ka signal hai.

Q2: Double Bottom pattern mein entry kahan leni chahiye?

Double bottom mein safe entry tab hoti hai jab neckline (highest point between two bottoms) break ho jaye aur close ho uske upar.

Scalping Strategies

Scalping mein chote-chote price movements se profit kamaya jaata hai — kai baar sirf 5-10 points ka target. Ye fastest trading style hai jismein speed aur discipline sab se zaroori hai.

1-Minute aur 5-Minute Scalping

Scalping ke liye sab se chote timeframes use hote hain. 1-minute chart par entries milti hain lekin bohot fast hona padta hai. 5-minute chart thoda stable hai aur beginners ke liye behtar hai.

SCALPING TIMEFRAME RULE
Entry: 1min ya 5min chart
Trend Confirmation: 15min chart
Higher Bias: 1hr chart Scalping mein 3 timeframe rule follow karein. sirf 1min chart par trade karna gambling hai.

Quick Entries aur Exits

Scalping mein entry aur exit dono bahut fast hote hain. Aik trade 2-15 minute tak hold hota hai. Key rules:

  • Entry: EMA bounce, VWAP bounce, ya order block par entry
  • Target: 5-15 points (forex) ya 0.1-0.3% (crypto)
  • Stop Loss: 3-8 points (forex) ya 0.05-0.15% (crypto)
  • Time Limit: Agar 15 minute mein target hit na ho, manually close karein

Spread Management

Scalping mein spread (bid-ask difference) bohot important hai. Agar spread zyada hai toh scalping profitable nahi hogi.

SPREAD RULE FOR SCALPING

BTC/USDT Spread0.01-0.05% — GOOD
Exotic Pairs Spread2-10 pips — AVOID scalping
Low Volume Altcoins0.5-2% — NEVER scalp

Best Pairs/Assets for Scalping

TOP SCALPING ASSETS

GBP/USDGood — Volatile, acche moves milte hain
BTC/USDTGood — High volume, tight spread

PRACTICE QUESTIONS

Q1: Scalping mein ek trade kitne tak hold hota hai?

Scalping mein trades bahut fast hote hain — 2 se 15 minute tak hold hota hai. 15 minute se zyada hold karna scalping nahi rehti.

Q2: Scalping ke liye sab se zaroori cheez kya hai?

Scalping mein profit chota hota hai isliye spread kam hona aur execution fast hona zaroori hai. Spread zyada hua toh profit khatam ho jayega.

Risk Management for Day Traders

Risk management day trading ki sab se zaroori skill hai. Bina risk management ke aap kitni bhi strategy seekh lein, eventually paisa gawa denge. Ye chapter aap ki trading ko safe banayega.

1-2% Risk Rule Per Trade

Ye rule sab se important hai: Har trade mein sirf 1-2% apne total account se risk karein. Agar aap ke paas $1000 hain, toh ek trade mein maximum $10-$20 ka loss hona chahiye.

1% RISK RULE
Account Balance: $1,000
Risk Per Trade: 1% = $10
Maximum Loss Per Day: 3% = $30 Ye rule tootne par account ki death hoti hai. Koi bhi trade itna risk na le k account 10%+ down ho jaye.

Position Sizing Formula

Position size nikalna zaroori hai taake pata ho k kitna lot ya quantity trade karna hai.

POSITION SIZE FORMULA
Position Size = (Account × Risk%) ÷ (Entry Price − Stop Loss Price) Example: $1,000 account, 1% risk, Entry $50,000, SL $49,800 Position Size = ($1,000 × 0.01) ÷ ($50,000 − $49,800) = $10 ÷ $200 = 0.05 BTC

Stop Loss Placement Strategies

  • Structure-based: Recent swing low/high ke neeche/upar
  • ATR-based: 1.5x ATR value entry se door
  • Percentage-based: Entry se 1-2% door (fixed)
  • Time-based: Agar specified time mein target na mile, close

Risk:Reward Minimum 1:2

Har trade mein minimum 1:2 Risk:Reward ratio hona chahiye. Yani agar $10 risk le rahe hain toh kam se kam $20 ka target hona chahiye.

RISK:REWARD EXAMPLE
Entry Price$50,000
Stop Loss$49,800 (Risk = $200)
Take Profit$50,400 (Reward = $400)
Risk:Reward1:2 ✅

Daily Loss Limit

Agar din mein 3-5% account se loss ho jaye toh trading band karein. Aaj ka din khatam. Kal nayi soch ke saath aayein.

! Daily Loss Limit Rule: Agar aap ka account $1,000 hai aur aaj $30-$50 (3-5%) loss ho gaya — abhi band karein. Revenge trading mat karein. Market kal bhi rahegi.

Trading Psychology — Day Trader Edition

Trading 80% psychology hai aur 20% strategy. Aap kitni bhi acchi strategy jaante hon, agar emotions control nahi kar paate toh consistently profit karna mumkin nahi.

FOMO (Fear of Missing Out)

FOMO tab hota hai jab aap dekhte hain k price tezi se barh rahi hai aur aap sochte hain "abhi enter karta hoon, mujhe bhi profit chahiye." Ye emotion sab se zyada traders ko taba karta hai.

  • FOMO mein li gayi trade aksar top par hoti hai
  • Price already 5% barh chuki hai, aap 6% par entry lete hain, phir 3% gir jati hai
  • Solution: Sirf apne setup ka wait karein. Agar setup nahi bana, trade mat lein.

Revenge Trading

Revenge trading tab hota hai jab loss ke baad aap turant wapas trade lete hain paisa wapas kamane ke liye. Ye sab se dangerous cycle hai.

  • Loss hua → Gussa aaya → Turant trade liya → Aur loss hua
  • Ek ghante mein 5-6 trades lena = revenge trading
  • Solution: Loss ke baad 30 minute ka break lein. Screen se uthein.

Overtrading

Overtrading tab hota hai jab bina kisi clear signal ke baar baar trade lete hain. Boredom, lalach, ya frustration ki wajah se hota hai.

  • Din mein 20-30 trades lena = overtrading
  • Har candle par entry lena = overtrading
  • Solution: Maximum 3-5 trades per day ka rule banayein.

Greed vs Fear Cycle

Ye cycle har trader ke saath hoti hai:

  1. Greed: Jab profit ho raha hai toh sochte hain "aur barhao"
  2. Fear: Jab price thoda neeche aaye toh dar jaate hain aur profit book kar lete hain
  3. Regret: Baad mein dekhte hain price aur barh gayi thi
  4. Greed (phir se): Next time zyada der hold karte hain, lekin price gir jaati hai

Discipline aur Routine

Successful traders ka ek hi common factor hai — discipline. Har din ek hi routine follow karte hain chahe market kuch bhi kar raha ho.

5:00 → Review aur next day ki tayyari Routine fix hona discipline banata hai. Har din ek hi jagah se trading karein.

Trading Journal Importance

Trading journal mein har trade ka record rakhein — entry/exit, reason, emotion, result. Ye aap ko patterns dikhata hai k aap kahan galti kar rahe hain.

Tips to Stay Disciplined

  1. Trading Plan Likhein: Sab kuch likha hona chahiye — entry, exit, risk, size.
  2. Journal Maintain Karein: Har trade ka record rakhein.
  3. Max Trades Set Karein: Din mein sirf 3-5 trades.
  4. Daily Loss Limit: 3% cross karein toh band.
  5. Break Lein: Loss ke baad 30 min break.
  6. Physical Health: Exercise, neend, khana — sab important hai.
  7. Screen Time Limit: 6-8 ghante se zyada na baithen.
  8. Emotions Notice Karein: Jab lalach ya dar mehsoos karein, trade band karein.

FOMO (Fear of Missing Out) mein trader bina setup ke trade le leta hai kyunki use lagta hai wo profit miss kar raha hai.

Q2: Revenge trading se kaise bachein?

Revenge trading se bachne ka sab se accha tareeqa hai break lena aur apne original trading plan par wapas aana.

Complete Trading Plan

Ye chapter aap ke liye ek complete trading plan template hai. Agar aap ne ye plan follow kiya toh aap systematically trading karenge bina emotions ke.

Pre-Market Routine (30 Minute Before)

  1. Market news check karein (forex factory, coin calendar)
  2. Higher timeframe (1hr/4hr) par trend identify karein

  • Entry signal mila hai (EMA crossover/breakout/pullback)?
  • Volume se confirmation hai?
  • Risk:Reward 1:2 ya us se zyada hai?
  • Stop loss set hai?
  • Position size calculate hai (1-2% risk)?
