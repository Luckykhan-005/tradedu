Our story

Three generations.
One recipe book.

La Tavola began as a six-table trattoria in 1998, built around recipes carried from Naples. Today we still roll every pasta by hand, source tomatoes from the same family farm, and pour wine from producers we know by name.

Come for the food, stay for the feeling of being genuinely welcome.

— Chef Marco Benedetti
