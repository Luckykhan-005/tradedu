Chart Patterns Kya Hain?

Chart patterns price charts pe bante hain jab buyers aur sellers ek doosre se larrte hain. Jab ye larrai ek fixed shape banati hai jaise triangle, head, ya double top — toh us shape ko chart pattern kehte hain.

Ye patterns is liye kaam karte hain kyunke har market mein insaani psychology same hoti hai. Jab price ek resistance tak pahunchti hai, log profit lena shuru karte hain. Jab support tak aati hai, log khareedna shuru karte hain. Ye repeat hone wali harkat patterns banaati hai.

Reversal vs Continuation Patterns

Kyun Kaam Karte Hain

Ye patterns psychology pe kaam karte hain. Jab price ek level se baar baar reject hoti hai, toh wo level resistance ban jaata hai. Jab baar baar support milta hai, toh wo level demand zone ban jaata hai. Ye levels break hone pe price tezi se move karti hai — isi ko breakout kehte hain.

Pattern Psychology: Buyer vs Seller Battle

Har pattern ke peeche ek kahani hai. Jab buyers strong hain toh price upar jaati hai. Jab sellers dominate karte hain toh price neeche girti hai. Patterns tab bante hain jab dono side ek doosre ke barabar hote hain aur phir koi jeetta hai.

  • Resistance Test: Price bar bar upar se reject hoti hai — sellers strong hain
  • Support Test: Price bar bar neeche se bounce karti hai — buyers strong hain
  • Breakout: Jab koi side haar jaata hai, price tezi se us direction mein jaati hai
  • Volume: Breakout pe volume barhna zaroori hai — warna fake breakout ho sakta hai
— Warning: Pattern sirf probability hai, guarantee nahi. 100% accuracy wala koi bhi pattern nahi hai. Hamesha stop loss lagayein aur risk management follow karein.

01

Chart Patterns Ki Dunya

Is chapter mein hum chart patterns ki poori dunya ko dekhenge — reversal patterns, continuation patterns, aur unki psychology.

Chart Patterns Kya Hain?

Chart patterns price action ki visual representation hain. Jab price ek particular shape banati hai chart pe, toh wo shape humein batati hai k buyers ya sellers mein se kaun jeet raha hai. Ye patterns technical analysis ka sab se important hissa hain.

Ye patterns tab bante hain jab market consolidation mein hota hai — yaani price ek range mein move karti hai aur phir breakout karti hai. Ye breakout humein trading opportunity deta hai.

Reversal Patterns

Reversal patterns tab bante hain jab existing trend khatam hone wala ho aur naya trend shuru hone wala ho. In mein:

  • Head & Shoulders: Sab se powerful bearish reversal pattern. Uptrend ke end pe banta hai.
  • Inverse Head & Shoulders: Bullish reversal pattern. Downtrend ke end pe banta hai.
  • Double Top: M-shape pattern. Uptrend ke end pe banta hai — bearish signal.
  • Double Bottom: W-shape pattern. Downtrend ke end pe banta hai — bullish signal.
  • Triple Top: Double top se bhi powerful. Teen equal highs banate hain.
  • Triple Bottom: Double bottom se bhi powerful. Teen equal lows banate hain.
  • Rounding Bottom: Slow reversal pattern. Price ek bowl shape banata hai.

Continuation Patterns

Continuation patterns tab bante hain jab trend ek time ke liye rukta hai (consolidation) aur phir usi direction mein continue karta hai. In mein:

  • Ascending Triangle: Bullish continuation. Flat top aur rising bottom.
  • Descending Triangle: Bearish continuation. Flat bottom aur falling top.
  • Symmetrical Triangle: Neutral — dono taraf breakout ho sakta hai.
  • Rising Wedge: Uptrend mein bearish reversal, downtrend mein continuation.
  • Falling Wedge: Downtrend mein bullish reversal, uptrend mein continuation.
  • Flags & Pennants: Short-term continuation patterns jo strong move ke baad bante hain.

Psychology Behind Patterns

Har pattern ke peeche buyer-seller ki battle hai. Jab market mein ek trend hota hai (uptrend ya downtrend), toh ek waqt aata hai jab woh trend thak jaata hai. Buyers ya sellers exhausted ho jaate hain aur doosri taraf taqat aati hai.

Ye exhaustion patterns banaati hai. Jaise:

  • Higher Highs ruk jaate hain — uptrend mein sellers aa rahe hain
  • Lower Lows ruk jaate hain — downtrend mein buyers aa rahe hain
  • Consolidation — dono taraf ki taqat barabar hai, abhi pata nahi
  • Breakout — koi ek side jeetta hai, price us taraf tezi se jaati hai
TRADING RULE
Pattern tab tak mat trade karo jab tak breakout na ho jayeBreakout ke bina pattern sirf drawin hai. Patience rakhein — breakout ka wait karein.

Timeframes Ka Role

Chart patterns har timeframe pe bante hain — 1 minute se le kar monthly charts tak. Lekin:

  • Higher Timeframes (4H, Daily, Weekly): Patterns zyada reliable hote hain
  • Lower Timeframes (1m, 5m, 15m): Patterns chhote hain, false breakouts zyada milte hain
  • Sab se acha approach: Daily chart pe pattern pehchano, execution ke liye 4H ya 1H use karo
› Pro Tip: Jab aap koi pattern pehchano, toh hamesha multiple timeframes check karein. Agar daily chart pe pattern hai aur 4H pe bhi confirm ho raha hai, toh signal zyada strong hai.
PRACTICE QUESTIONS
Q1. Kaunsa pattern reversal nahi hai?
Sahi jawab: Ascending Triangle ek continuation pattern hai, reversal pattern nahi. Ye generally bullish continuation signal deta hai jab flat resistance ke upar breakout hota hai.
Q2. Pattern trade karne ke liye sab se zaroori cheez kya hai?
Sahi jawab: Breakout ke bina pattern trade karna gambling hai. Hamesha breakout ka wait karein aur volume se confirm karein ke asli breakout hai ya fake.
02

Head & Shoulders

Head & Shoulders sab se powerful aur well-known reversal pattern hai. Jab ye banta hai, toh trend change hone ka signal milta hai.

Regular Head & Shoulders (Bearish Reversal)

Ye pattern uptrend ke end pe banta hai aur downtrend ki shuruat ka signal deta hai. Is mein teen peaks (tops) hote hain:

  • Left Shoulder: Pehla peak — uptrend mein banta hai, phir thoda correction aata hai
  • Head: Dusra peak — sab se ooncha hota hai, left shoulder se zyada
  • Right Shoulder: Teesra peak — head se chhota hota hai, left shoulder ke barabar ya thoda kam
  • Neckline: Support line jo left shoulder ke baad ke low aur head ke baad ke low ko join karti hai

Inverse Head & Shoulders (Bullish Reversal)

Ye pattern downtrend ke end pe banta hai aur uptrend ki shuruat ka signal deta hai. Is ka ulta hota hai — teen lows (bottoms) hote hain:

  • Left Shoulder: Pehla bottom — downtrend mein banta hai
  • Head: Dusra bottom — sab se neeche hota hai
  • Right Shoulder: Teesra bottom — head se ooncha hota hai
  • Neckline: Resistance line jo dono shoulders ke highs ko join karti hai

Neckline Kaise Draw Karein

Neckline draw karna simple hai:

  1. Left shoulder ke baad ka low point pehchano
  2. Head ke baad ka low point pehchano
  3. In dono points ko ek line se join karo
  4. Ye line neckline hai — is ke tootne pe sell signal milta hai

Neckline horizontal ho sakti hai ya slightly tilted. Dono cases mein kaam karti hai.

Target Calculation

TARGET FORMULA
Target = Neckline - (Head - Neckline)Yaani: Neckline se Head ki doori utna neeche target hota hai. Jitna ooncha Head, utna bada target.

Example: Agar neckline 100 pe hai aur head 130 pe hai, toh target = 100 - (130 - 100) = 70.

Volume Confirmation

Volume bahut important hai Head & Shoulders mein:

  • Left Shoulder: Volume normal ya thoda zyada
  • Head: Volume kam hona chahiye left shoulder se — yehi weakness ka sign hai
  • Right Shoulder: Volume sab se kam hona chahiye
  • Neckline Break: Volume barhna chahiye — asli breakout hai

Entry Rules

  • Aggressive Entry: Neckline tootte hi sell karo
  • Conservative Entry: Neckline tootne ke baad retest ka wait karo — jab price wapas neckline tak aaye aur reject ho
  • Stop Loss: Right shoulder ke upar lagao

Trading Rules Summary

HEAD & SHOULDERS TRADING PLAN
Pattern TypeBearish Reversal (Regular) / Bullish (Inverse)
Entry SignalNeckline break + Volume confirmation
Stop LossRight shoulder ke upar (Regular) / neeche (Inverse)
TargetNeckline - Head-Neckline distance
Volume CheckBreakout pe volume barha hona chahiye
TimeframeDaily ya 4H sab se reliable
PRACTICE QUESTIONS
Q1. Head & Shoulders pattern mein Head kaisa hota hai?
Sahi jawab: Head sab se ooncha peak hota hai — left aur right shoulders se zyada ooncha. Yehi pattern ko recognizable banata hai.
Q2. Agar neckline 200 pe hai aur head 250 pe hai, toh target kya hoga?
Sahi jawab: Target = Neckline - (Head - Neckline) = 200 - (250 - 200) = 200 - 50 = 150.
03

Double Top & Double Bottom

Double Top aur Double Bottom sab se commonly dekhe jaane wale reversal patterns hain. In ko M-shape aur W-shape bhi kehte hain.

Double Top (M Pattern) — Bearish Reversal

Double Top uptrend ke end pe banta hai aur downtrend ki shuruat ka signal deta hai. Is ki shakal M jaisi hoti hai:

  • Pehla Top: Price uptrend mein oonchi jaati hai aur resistance level pe rukti hai
  • Correction: Price thoda neeche aata hai support tak
  • Dusra Top: Price phir se upar jaati hai lekin pehle top ko cross nahi kar paati
  • Breakout: Jab support (neckline) tootta hai, price neeche girti hai

Double Bottom (W Pattern) — Bullish Reversal

Double Bottom downtrend ke end pe banta hai aur uptrend ki shuruat ka signal deta hai. Is ki shakal W jaisi hoti hai:

  • Pehla Bottom: Price downtrend mein neeche jaati hai aur support level pe rukti hai
  • Correction: Price thoda upar aata hai resistance tak
  • Dusra Bottom: Price phir se neeche jaati hai lekin pehle bottom ko cross nahi kar paati
  • Breakout: Jab resistance (neckline) tootta hai, price upar jaati hai

Confirmation: Neckline Break

Double patterns mein neckline bahut important hai. Pattern tab tak confirm nahi hota jab tak neckline na toot jaye. Price ko neckline ke neeche (Double Top) ya upar (Double Bottom) close karna hota hai.

Target Calculation

TARGET FORMULA
Double Top: Target = Neckline - (Top - Neckline)Double Bottom: Target = Neckline + (Neckline - Bottom)
Yaani: Top/Bottom se neckline ki doori utna target hota hai.

Volume Analysis During Formation

  • Pehla Top/Bottom: Volume normal hota hai
  • Dusra Top/Bottom: Volume pehle se kam hota hai — yehi weakness ka sign hai
  • Neckline Break: Volume barhna chahiye — asli breakout hai
  • Volume kam hai breakout pe: Fake breakout ka chance hai — bach ke rahein

Entry and Stop Loss Rules

DOUBLE TOP/BOTTOM TRADING PLAN
EntryNeckline break ke baad retest ka wait karo ya break ke turant baad
Stop Loss (Top)Right top ke upar
Stop Loss (Bottom)Right bottom ke neeche
TargetPattern ki height neckline se add/subtract karo
Risk:RewardKam se kam 1:2 hona chahiye
PRACTICE QUESTIONS
Q1. Double Top ki shakal kaisi hoti hai?
Sahi jawab: Double Top ki shakal M jaisi hoti hai — do equal highs banate hain jo resistance pe ruk jaate hain. Is liye is ko M-pattern bhi kehte hain.
Q2. Double Top mein second top pe volume kaisa hota hai?
Sahi jawab: Second top pe volume kam hota hai — ye weakness ka sign hai. Agar buyers strong hote toh volume zyada hota aur price pehle top ko cross kar leti.
04

Triple Top & Triple Bottom

Triple Top aur Triple Bottom double patterns ka advanced version hain. Ye zyada powerful hain kyunke teen dafa resistance/support test hone ke baad breakout zyada strong hota hai.

Triple Top — Three Equal Highs

Triple Top uptrend ke end pe banta hai. Is mein price teen dafa ek hi resistance level pe rukti hai aur phir neeche girti hai:

  • Teen Tops: Price teen dafa lagatar resistance tak jaati hai lekin cross nahi kar paati
  • Neckline: Support level jo teeno lows ko connect karta hai
  • Breakout: Jab neckline tootta hai, price tezi se neeche jaati hai
  • Volume: Har top ke sath volume kam hota jaata hai — yehi weakness ka sign hai

Triple Bottom — Three Equal Lows

Triple Bottom downtrend ke end pe banta hai. Price teen dafa ek hi support level pe rukti hai aur phir upar jaati hai:

  • Teen Bottoms: Price teen dafa lagatar support tak jaati hai lekin neeche nahi ja paati
  • Neckline: Resistance level jo teeno highs ko connect karta hai
  • Breakout: Jab neckline tootta hai, price tezi se upar jaati hai
  • Volume: Har bottom ke sath volume kam hota jaata hai

Kyun Stronger Hain Double Se

Triple patterns double patterns se is liye strong hain kyunke:

  • 3 Dafa Test: Resistance ya support 3 dafa test ho chuka hai — yehi level bahut strong hai
  • Thakaan: Buyers ya sellers 3 dafa haar chuke hain — unki taqat khatam ho rahi hai
  • Zyada Confirmation: 3 peaks/lows dekh kar confirm karna asaan hota hai
  • Bigger Move: Breakout ke baad move zyada bada hota hai kyunki zyada logon ne is level pe trade kiya hota hai

Target Calculation

TARGET FORMULA
Triple Top: Target = Neckline - (Top - Neckline)Triple Bottom: Target = Neckline + (Neckline - Bottom)
Pattern ki height se target nikalte hain — same as Double patterns.

Volume Declining During Formation

Triple patterns mein volume ek key indicator hai:

  • First Peak/Trough: Sab se zyada volume
  • Second Peak/Trough: Volume kam hota hai
  • Third Peak/Trough: Volume sab se kam hota hai
  • Breakout: Volume suddenly barhta hai — asli move shuru hua

Ye declining volume pattern ye confirm karta hai k trend khatam ho raha hai aur naya trend shuru hone wala hai.

Trading Rules

TRIPLE TOP/BOTTOM TRADING PLAN
EntryNeckline break + Volume spike ka wait karo
Stop Loss (Top)Third top ke upar — ye sab se safe jagah hai
Stop Loss (Bottom)Third bottom ke neeche
TargetPattern ki height neckline se door
Risk:RewardKam se kam 1:2, ideally 1:3
ConfirmationCandle close zaroori hai breakout ke baad
PRACTICE QUESTIONS
Q1. Triple Top mein kitne peaks hote hain?
Sahi jawab: Triple Top mein teen equal peaks hote hain jo resistance level pe ruk jaate hain. Is ko teen dafa test karna yehi pattern ko special banata hai.
Q2. Triple patterns double se kyun strong hain?
Sahi jawab: Jab koi level 3 dafa test ho chuka hai aur phir tootta hai, toh us level pe bahut sare orders hote hain jo ek sath trigger hote hain — is liye breakout zyada powerful hota hai.
05

Triangles (Ascending, Descending, Symmetrical)

Triangles sab se commonly dekhe jaane wale continuation patterns hain. Ye jab bante hain toh price consolidation mein hoti hai aur phir ek direction mein break karti hai.

Ascending Triangle (Bullish)

Ascending triangle mein ek flat resistance hota hai aur rising support hota hai:

  • Flat Top: Price bar bar ek hi resistance level pe rukti hai
  • Rising Bottom: Har baar price neeche se zyada oonchi jagah pe rukti hai — buyers strong hain
  • Breakout: Zyada tar breakout upar hota hai — resistance tootta hai
  • Volume: Triangle ke andar volume kam hota hai, breakout pe barhta hai

Descending Triangle (Bearish)

Descending triangle mein ek flat support hota hai aur falling resistance hota hai:

  • Flat Bottom: Price bar bar ek hi support level pe rukti hai
  • Falling Top: Har baar price upar se zyada neeche rukti hai — sellers strong hain
  • Breakout: Zyada tar breakout neeche hota hai — support tootta hai
  • Volume: Triangle ke andar volume kam hota hai, breakout pe barhta hai

Symmetrical Triangle (Neutral)

Symmetrical triangle mein dono lines converge karti hain — na resistance flat hai na support:

  • Converging Lines: Upar wali line neeche aa rahi hai, neeche wali line upar ja rahi hai
  • Breakout Direction: Dono taraf ho sakta hai — upar ya neeche
  • Trend Bias: Agar pehle uptrend tha toh zyada tar breakout upar hota hai, aur vice versa
  • Volume: Triangle ke andar volume consistently kam hota hai

Breakout Direction and Timing

Triangles mein breakout ka time aur direction bahut important hai:

  • Timing: Triangle ke 2/3 hisse tak breakout hona chahiye — agar apex tak wait karo toh weak breakout milega
  • Volume Spike: Breakout pe volume barha hona chahiye — warna fake hai
  • Candle Close: breakout ke baad candle close hona zaroori hai — sirf wick nahi chalegi
  • Retest: Breakout ke baad price retest karti hai toh aur bhi strong signal hai

Target Calculation

TARGET FORMULA
Target = Triangle ki height + Breakout pointYaani: Triangle ke sab se oonche point se sab se neeche point ki doori, us ko breakout point pe add (ya subtract) karo.

Volume: Declining During Formation

Sab hi triangles mein volume declining hota hai jab tak breakout na ho. Ye normal hai kyunke consolidation mein traders wait karte hain. Jab breakout hota hai toh sab ek sath enter karte hain — is liye volume spike hota hai.

  • Ascending: Breakout upar ka volume zyada hota hai
  • Descending: Breakout neeche ka volume zyada hota hai
  • Symmetrical: Dono taraf ka breakout volume-dependent hai
› Pro Tip: Triangle ke apex ke bilkul qareeb breakout weak hota hai. Agar price apex tak aa gayi hai aur breakout nahi hua, toh behtar hai wait karein aur naya pattern dekhein.
PRACTICE QUESTIONS
Q1. Ascending triangle mein breakout zyada tar kis direction mein hota hai?
Sahi jawab: Ascending triangle mein flat resistance hai aur rising support hai — matlab buyers har baar zyada strong ho rahe hain. Is liye breakout zyada tar upar hota hai.
Q2. Triangle ke andar volume kaisa hota hai?
Sahi jawab: Triangle ke andar volume consistently kam hota hai kyunke consolidation mein traders wait karte hain. Volume sirf breakout pe barhta hai.
06

Wedges (Rising & Falling)

Wedges triangles jaise dikhte hain lekin un mein ek major farq hai — dono lines same direction mein tilt hoti hain. Wedges zyada tar reversal signals dete hain.

Rising Wedge (Bearish)

Rising wedge mein dono lines upar ki taraf jati hain lekin convergence hoti hai. Ye pattern do situations mein milta hai:

  • Uptrend ke end pe: Bearish reversal signal — price neeche girne wali hai
  • Downtrend ke beech mein: Continuation signal — price neeche jaayegi

Rising wedge mein resistance line support line se zyada steep hoti hai — is liye dono converge karti hain aur price squeeze hoti hai.

Falling Wedge (Bullish)

Falling wedge mein dono lines neeche ki taraf jati hain lekin convergence hoti hai:

  • Downtrend ke end pe: Bullish reversal signal — price upar jaane wali hai
  • Uptrend ke beech mein: Continuation signal — price upar jaayegi

Falling wedge mein support line resistance line se zyada steep hoti hai — buyers har baar zyada jaldi aa rahe hain.

Wedge vs Triangle: Kya Farq Hai

WEDGE vs TRIANGLE
WedgeDono lines same direction mein tilt hoti hain
TriangleEk line flat hoti hai ya dono converge karti hain
Wedge SignalZyada tar reversal signal deta hai
Triangle SignalZyada tar continuation signal deta hai
Wedge SlopeDono lines ka slope alag hota hai
Triangle SlopeKam se kam ek line flat hoti hai

Breakout Timing

Wedges mein breakout ka time triangle se thoda alag hota hai:

  • Wedge ke andar: Price multiple touches karti hai support/resistance pe — kam se kam 5-6 touches zaroori hain
  • Breakout point: Jab wedge ki dono lines ka gap bahut chhota ho jaaye — us se pehle breakout hona chahiye
  • Time limit: Agar wedge 3-4 hafton se zyada lamba ho gaya hai toh signal weak hota hai

Target Calculation

TARGET FORMULA
Target = Wedge ki beginning ki height - Breakout pointYaani: Wedge ke sab se pehle point ki doori se target milta hai. Jitna bada wedge, utna bada target.

Volume Analysis

Wedges mein volume triangle se thoda different hota hai:

  • Formation ke dauran: Volume gradually kam hota hai — same as triangles
  • Rising Wedge: Uptrend mein volume barh raha hota hai lekin wedge ke andar kam ho raha hai — bearish sign
  • Falling Wedge: Downtrend mein volume barh raha hota hai lekin wedge ke andar kam ho raha hai — bullish sign
  • Breakout pe: Volume spike hona chahiye — same as other patterns

Entry and Stop Loss Rules

WEDGE TRADING PLAN
Rising Wedge EntrySupport line ke neeche close hone pe sell karo
Falling Wedge EntryResistance line ke upar close hone pe buy karo
Stop Loss (Rising)Wedge ke andar last swing high ke upar
Stop Loss (Falling)Wedge ke andar last swing low ke neeche
TargetWedge ki beginning ki height
Risk:RewardKam se kam 1:2
ConfirmationVolume spike + candle close zaroori
› Important Note: Rising Wedge hamesha bearish nahi hota. Agar ye uptrend ke beech mein continuation ke taur pe banta hai, toh iska matlab hai k price thoda neeche aayegi phir se upar jaayegi. Context bahut important hai!
PRACTICE QUESTIONS
Q1. Falling wedge kis direction ka signal deta hai?
Sahi jawab: Falling wedge bullish reversal pattern hai. Jab ye downtrend ke end pe banta hai toh price upar jaane ka signal milta hai — buyers gradually strong ho rahe hain.
Q2. Wedge aur triangle mein kya farq hai?
Sahi jawab: Wedge mein dono lines ek hi taraf tilt hoti hain (dono upar ya dono neeche) jabke triangle mein kam se kam ek line flat hoti hai ya dono symmetrically converge karti hain. Is farq se signals bhi alag hote hain.
07

Flags & Pennants

Flags aur pennants continuation patterns hain — strong move ke baad chhota consolidation hota hai phir trend continue hota hai.

Bull Flag

Uptrend mein strong up-move ke baad price thoda pullback karta hai (flag shape). Phir breakout hota hai upar.

  • Flagpole: Strong up-move (jahan se pattern shuru hua)
  • Flag: Chhota downward sloping rectangle
  • Breakout: Flag ke upar close hona
  • Target: Flagpole ki height = breakout se target
TARGET FORMULA
Target = Breakout Price + Flagpole Height

Bear Flag

Downtrend mein strong down-move ke baad price thoda pullback karta hai upar (flag). Phir breakout hota hai neeche.

  • Target: Flagpole ki height neeche se subtract karo
  • Volume: Flagpole pe high, flag pe declining, breakout pe spike

Pennants

Triangles jaise hote hain lekin chhote — strong move ke baad 1-3 weeks ka consolidation.

PRACTICE QUESTION
Bull Flag mein target kaise nikalte hain?
Flagpole ki height (strong move) ko breakout point pe add karte hain.
08

Cup and Handle

Cup and Handle ek bullish continuation pattern hai — U-shaped recovery ke baad chhota pullback (handle) aata hai phir breakout hota hai.

Pattern Structure

  • Cup: U-shaped recovery — 7 se 65 weeks tak banta hai
  • Handle: Chhota pullback (1-4 weeks) cup ke right side pe
  • Breakout: Handle ke upar close hona on high volume
  • Target: Cup ki depth = breakout se target
TARGET FORMULA
Target = Breakout Price + Cup Depth
Cup Depth = Cup High - Cup Low

Inverse Cup and Handle

Bearish version — upside-down cup aur handle. Downtrend continuation signal hai.

PRACTICE QUESTION
Cup ka ideal duration kitna hota hai?
Cup ideally 7 se 65 weeks mein banta hai. Jitna gehra aur lamba cup, utna powerful pattern.
09

Rounding Bottom (Saucer)

Rounding Bottom ek long-term bullish reversal pattern hai — gradually sellers se buyers ka control hota hai.

Pattern Structure

  • Price slowly U-shape banata hai
  • Volume: High at start → Low in middle → High at end
  • Duration: Weeks se months tak
  • Breakout: Neckline level ke upar close on high volume

Cup and Handle se fark

  • Rounding Bottom mein handle nahi hota
  • Duration zyada hota hai
  • Same target formula use hota hai
PRACTICE QUESTION
Rounding Bottom mein volume kaise behave karta hai?
Volume start pe high, middle mein low, aur end pe phir high hota hai — U-shape volume pattern.

Breakout & Retest Strategy

Breakout ke baad price often retest karta hai broken level ko — ye safest entry point hai.

Valid Breakout Kaise Pehchanein

  • Price close ho broken level ke upar/neeche (sirf wick nahi)
  • Volume spike ho breakout pe
  • Multiple candles close beyond level

Retest Strategy

  1. Breakout ka wait karein
  2. Retest ka wait karein (price broken level pe wapas aaye)
  3. Retest pe reversal candle dekhein (pin bar, engulfing)
  4. Entry lein with stop loss beyond the level
  5. Target: Next resistance/support ya measured move
ENTRY RULE
Retest Entry = Broken level + Reversal confirmation
Stop Loss = Just beyond the retested level

Fakeout se Kaise Bachein

  • Sirf wick ke breakout pe enter na karein — candle close ka wait karein
  • Volume confirm karein — low volume breakout suspicious hai
  • Retest ka wait karein — sabse safe approach
PRACTICE QUESTION
Breakout ke baad sabse safe entry kab hai?
Retest ke baad entry sabse safe hai kyunki aapko confirmation milta hai ke level hold ho raha hai.

Pattern Failures & Fakeouts

Har pattern 100% kaam nahi karta — failures aur fakeouts se bachna seekho.

Pattern Fail Kyun Hota Hai

  • Market structure change ho jata hai
  • Bada news/event aata hai
  • Low volume pe breakout hota hai
  • Pattern galat identify kiya gaya

Fakeout Signs

  1. Breakout pe volume kam hai
  2. Price quickly reverse ho jaata hai
  3. Sirf wick beyond level jaata hai, candle close nahi
  4. Pattern perfect nahi ban raha

Stop Loss Kahan Lagayein

  • Pattern ke opposite side pe
  • Right shoulder ke upar (H&S ke liye)
  • Neckline ke neeche (Double Top ke liye)
PRACTICE QUESTION
Fakeout ka sabse bada sign kya hai?
Fakeout mein volume kam hota hai aur price quickly reverse ho jaata hai — real breakout mein high volume aur sustained move hota hai.
