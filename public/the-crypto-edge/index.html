Process-Driven Trader ka Mindset

Indicators tools hain. Mindset decide karta hai ke woh tools sahi use ho rahe hain ya nahi.

Process-driven trader single trade ke result pe nahi, balki decision ki quality pe focus karta hai. Market uncertain hai. Best setups bhi fail ho sakte hain. Jo cheez aap control kar sakte ho woh hai aapka process.

Core Principles

  • Process over Outcome – Trade jeetna ya harna control nahi kar sakte. Rules follow karna control kar sakte ho.
  • Uncertainty Accept Karo – Indicators probability badhate hain, risk khatam nahi karte.
  • Emotional Neutrality – Badi win ke baad overconfident mat bano. Loss ke baad revenge trading mat karo.

Har trade se pehle yeh 3 sawaal poocho:

2. Kya risk clearly defined aur acceptable hai?
3. Kya main abhi emotionally calm hoon?

Agar kisi ek ka jawab “No” hai to trade skip kar do.
Key Takeaway: Successful trading pehle dimaag mein hoti hai, chart pe baad mein.

Chapter 3

Risk Management: Asli Edge

Koi bhi indicator us trader ko nahi bacha sakta jo ek trade pe bohot zyada risk leta hai.

Risk management asli edge is liye hai kyunki yeh aapko market mein itni der alive rakhta hai ke aapka edge kaam kar sake.

Is Book ke Basic Rules

  • Har trade pe capital ka sirf 0.5% se 1% risk lo
  • Minimum risk-reward 1:1.5 rakho (prefer 1:2)
  • Position size stop-loss ki distance se calculate karo
  • Losing streak ke baad position size kabhi mat badhao
  • ATR use karke realistic stops set karo
1% risk per trade wala trader lambi losing streak bhi survive kar sakta hai. 5-10% risk wala aksar nahi kar pata.
Key Takeaway: Risk management optional nahi hai. Yeh har strategy ki foundation hai.

Chapter 4

RSI: Momentum ka Badshah

Relative Strength Index (RSI) recent price changes ki speed aur magnitude measure karta hai. Crypto mein yeh sab se reliable momentum tools mein se ek hai.

Standard setting: 14-period

Key levels:

  • 70 se upar → Overbought zone
  • 30 se neeche → Oversold zone
  • 50 level → Momentum shift line

Professional Guidelines

  • Sirf is liye buy/sell mat karo ke RSI overbought ya oversold hai
  • Divergence dekho: price new high banaye aur RSI lower high → bearish divergence
  • 50 level ko filter ki tarah use karo. Higher timeframe pe RSI 50 ke upar ho to long bias better hai
Key Takeaway: RSI ko akela mat use karo. Trend aur volatility tools ke saath combine karo.

Volume & OBV: Sachai ka Detector

Price jhoot bol sakti hai. Volume bohot mushkil se jhoot bolta hai.

  • Falling OBV + rising price = distribution

Chapter 8

Bollinger Bands: Volatility & Squeeze Mastery

Bollinger Bands is book ka core volatility tool hain.

Structure:

  • Middle Band = 20-period SMA
  • Upper Band = Middle + (2 × Standard Deviation)
  • Lower Band = Middle – (2 × Standard Deviation)

Jab bands wide hoti hain to volatility high hoti hai. Jab bands tight ho kar almost chipak jati hain to us condition ko Squeeze kehte hain.

Squeeze ka matlab hai ke energy build ho rahi hai. Iske baad aksar strong directional move aata hai.

TradeEd Squeeze Breakout Rules

Bullish Setup:

  1. Clear Squeeze form ho (bands tight)
  2. Price Upper Band se upar strong candle close kare
  3. Volume significantly increase ho
  4. RSI 50 ke upar ho
  5. Higher timeframe trend bullish ho

Bearish Setup: Bilkul opposite conditions.

Stop Loss: Opposite band ke thoda bahar ya ATR based.
Targets: Pehla target opposite band, phir 1.5x–2x risk. Trailing ke liye middle band use karo.

Key Takeaway: Bollinger Bands volume aur momentum confirmation ke saath sab se powerful hote hain.

Chapter 12

Complete Trading Workflow

Step-by-step process:

  1. Higher-timeframe trend bias check karo
  2. Trading timeframe pe Bollinger squeezes scan karo
  3. Clear breakout candle + volume ka wait karo
  4. RSI aur MACD se momentum confirm karo
  5. ATR based stop se position size calculate karo
  6. Breakout close ya next open pe enter karo
  7. Stop-loss aur initial targets set karo
  8. Middle band ko trailing reference banao
  9. Har trade ko journal mein record karo (result se independent)

Chapter 14

Trade Journaling & Continuous Improvement

Trading journal woh feedback system hai jo experience ko skill mein badalta hai.

Har trade pe minimum yeh record karo:

  • Date aur pair
  • Setup type (e.g. Bollinger squeeze breakout)
  • Direction aur timeframe
  • Entry, stop, targets
  • Risk percentage aur risk-reward
  • Outcome (win/loss/breakeven)
  • Chart ka screenshot
  • Emotional state (pehle aur baad mein)
  • Ek lesson learned

Journal ko weekly review karo. Winning aur losing dono trades mein patterns dhoondo. Rules tabhi change karo jab sufficient sample size ho, chand results ke baad nahi.

Key Takeaway: Bina journal ke improvement almost impossible hai.
