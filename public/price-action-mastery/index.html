Chapter 2

Support & Resistance ki Bunyad

Support woh level hai jahan buyers ne pehle price ko neeche jaane se roka. Resistance woh level hai jahan sellers ne price ko upar jaane se roka.

Jab price kisi level se multiple times bounce karti hai, to woh level strong ban jata hai. Jitni baar test hota hai aur hold karta hai, utna zyada significant maana jata hai.

Figure 1: Price Support se bounce karti hai, Resistance se reject hoti hai
Key Takeaway: Support = Buyers ka zone. Resistance = Sellers ka zone. Multiple touches = Stronger level.

Chapter 3

Clean Support & Resistance Kaise Draw Karein

Clean levels draw karna ek skill hai. Bohot se traders har chhoti wick pe line bana dete hain, jis se chart messy ho jata hai.

Best practices:

  • Higher timeframe pe pehle major levels identify karo (4H / Daily)
  • Wicks ke extreme se zyada body / close areas ko importance do
  • Kam az kam 2-3 clear touches wale levels ko hi major maano
  • Recent levels ko purane levels se zyada weight do
  • Round numbers (psychological levels) ko extra attention do
Rule: Agar level clear nahi dikh raha to usko force mat karo. Clean levels hi tradeable hote hain.
Key Takeaway: Less is more. Sirf clear, well-tested levels draw karo.

Chapter 4

Types of Support & Resistance

Teen common types:

  • Horizontal S/R – Seedhi horizontal lines (sab se common aur clean)
  • Trendline S/R – Uptrend ya downtrend ke along slanted lines
  • Dynamic S/R – Moving averages (jaise EMA 50/200) jo price ke saath move karti hain

Horizontal levels shuru mein sab se reliable maane jate hain kyunki woh clear aur objective hote hain. Trendlines subjective ho sakti hain. Dynamic levels trend ke dauran useful hote hain.

Key Takeaway: Pehle horizontal major levels master karo. Phir trendline aur dynamic add karo.

Chapter 5

Order Blocks Kya Hain

Order Block woh last opposing candle (ya candle cluster) hota hai jahan se price ne strong move start kiya.

  • Bullish Order Block → Last bearish (down) candle se pehle strong up move
  • Bearish Order Block → Last bullish (up) candle se pehle strong down move

Institutions aksar in zones se orders leave karte hain. Jab price wapas aati hai to yeh zones react kar sakte hain.

Figure 2: Bullish Order Block – Last down candle se strong up move, baad mein price wapas aati hai
Key Takeaway: Order Block = Strong move se pehle wali opposing candle zone.

Chapter 6

Bullish & Bearish Order Blocks

Bullish Order Block Trading

  • Strong bullish move identify karo
  • Us move se pehle wali last bearish candle (ya small cluster) mark karo
  • Price jab wapas us zone mein aaye to long bias
  • Confirmation: Rejection wick, bullish candle close, volume
  • Stop: Order Block ke neeche

Bearish Order Block Trading

  • Strong bearish move identify karo
  • Us move se pehle wali last bullish candle mark karo
  • Price jab wapas us zone mein aaye to short bias
  • Confirmation: Rejection, bearish close, volume
  • Stop: Order Block ke upar
Key Takeaway: Order Block = Zone. Confirmation candle ke baghair blindly enter mat karo.

Chapter 7

Fair Value Gaps (FVG) – Imbalance Concept

Fair Value Gap (FVG) tab banta hai jab price itni tezi se move karti hai ke beech mein imbalance (gap) reh jata hai. Yani ek candle ka high/low doosri relevant candle se overlap nahi karta.

Simple 3-candle structure:

  • Candle 1
  • Candle 2 (strong impulsive)
  • Candle 3

Agar Candle 1 aur Candle 3 ke beech gap reh jaye (unka high/low overlap na kare) to FVG form hota hai.

Figure 3: Bullish FVG – Candle 1 aur Candle 3 ke beech imbalance
Key Takeaway: FVG = Price imbalance. Market aksar is gap ko baad mein fill karne ki koshish karti hai.

Chapter 10

Entry, Stop Loss & Target Planning

Entry: Zone ke andar confirmation candle (rejection wick + close) ke baad.

Stop Loss: Zone ke thoda bahar (Order Block / FVG / Support ke neeche for long).

Targets:

  • Pehla target: Nearest opposite level / recent swing
  • Doosra target: Next major Resistance/Support
  • Partial profits pehle target pe, baqi trail

Risk-reward minimum 1:1.5 prefer karo. Agar stop bohot wide hai aur target qareeb hai to setup skip karna better hai.

Key Takeaway: Clear invalidation point (stop) ke baghair trade mat lo.

Chapter 11

Higher vs Lower Timeframe Confluence

Higher timeframe (4H / Daily) pe major S/R, Order Blocks aur FVGs identify karo. Lower timeframe (15m / 1H) pe entry refine karo.

Yeh approach is liye powerful hai kyunki:

  • Higher TF levels zyada significant hote hain
  • Lower TF pe precise entry milti hai
  • Risk better control hota hai
Pehle higher TF pe bias aur major zones, phir lower TF pe confirmation aur entry.
Key Takeaway: Multi-timeframe confluence = Higher quality setups.

Chapter 13

Complete Price Action Workflow
  1. Higher timeframe (4H/Daily) pe major Support, Resistance, Order Blocks aur FVGs mark karo
  2. Bias decide karo (bullish / bearish / neutral)
  3. Lower timeframe pe price ke un zones ki taraf aane ka wait karo
  4. Zone ke andar confirmation candle ka wait karo
  5. Volume aur simple momentum (RSI) se extra filter lo
  6. Stop zone ke bahar, target opposite levels pe set karo
  7. Risk 0.5–1% rakho
  8. Trade journal mein record karo

Pre-Trade Checklist

  • ☐ Higher TF major zone clear hai?
  • ☐ Bias us direction mein hai?
  • ☐ Order Block ya FVG overlap hai?
  • ☐ Confirmation candle aa gaya?
  • ☐ Risk-reward acceptable hai?
  • ☐ Risk 1% se zyada to nahi?
Key Takeaway: Workflow follow karna emotional decisions kam karta hai.
