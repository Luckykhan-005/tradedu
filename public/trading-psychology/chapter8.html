باب ۸: Professional Mindset

۱. Professional kaise sochta hai?

Har trade ek probability hai — koi sure nahi hai. Loss = business expense. Profit = bonus. Emotions ka koi kaam nahi.

۲. Mindset Shift

AmateurProfessional
Mujhe profit chahiyeMujhe rules follow karne hain
Ye trade sure haiYe ek setup hai — probability
Loss = failureLoss = learning
Mujhe har din trade karna haiSirf acche setup pe trade
Main market ko jeetungaMain market ke saath chalta hoon

۳. Acceptance

Accept karo — loss hoga — har din nahi hoga — 50% trades loss mein honge — ye normal hai. Long term mein profit chahiye — short term mein nahi.

۴. Growth Mindset

Har loss se seekho — "kya seekha" socho — "kya galti hui" mat socho. Improvement pe focus karo — results khud aayenge.

🔑 Ahm Nuktaat

Professional = rules + patience + consistency.

Har din nahi sochte — monthly sochte hain.

Market ko control nahi kar sakte — apne emotions control karein.
