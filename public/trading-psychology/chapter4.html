باب ۴: FOMO

۱. FOMO kya hai?

English: Fear Of Missing Out

Dosron ko profit hota dekh ke jaldi trade karna — "mujhe bhi chahiye" — bina setup ke entry.

۲. FOMO Examples

BTC $30K se $35K gaya — aapne nahi kharida — ab $36K pe "ab bhi jaayega" keh ke kharida — top pe aa gaya.

Coin 200% up hua — "ab bhi hoga" keh ke — late entry — loss.

۳. FOMO se Bachne ka Tarika

1. Market roz hai —机会 roz aayenge
2. Agar setup nahi hai — trade mat karo
3. Doosron ka profit dekh ke trade mat karo
4. Apna plan follow karo

۴. Reality

Jo log profit dikha rahe hain — wo bhi loss karte hain — sirf profit dikhate hain. Social media pe sab lucky dikhate hain — asliyat mein 90% loss karte hain.

🔑 Ahm Nuktaat

FOMO = late entry = loss — hamesha.

Agar miss ho gaya — agla机会 aayega — market kabhi band nahi hota.

Apne plan pe tike raho — dosron ki profit se na ghabrao.
