۱. Daily Psychological Rules

#Rule
۱Trade sirf plan ke mutabiq
۲2 loss ke baad — break
۳Loss ke baad — revenge na karo
۴Profit ke baad — overconfident na bano
۵FOMO se bacho — setup wait karo
۶Journal zaroor likho

۲. Monthly Review

Kitne rules follow kiye? Kitne emotions note kiye? Kya patterns mile? Kya improve karna hai?

۳. Growth Path

Month 1: Emotions pehchano | Month 2: Rules banao | Month 3: Rules follow karo | Month 6: Consistent bano

۴. Final Wisdom

Trading 80% psychology hai — 20% strategy. Pehle apne aap ko control karein — phir market control hogi. Sab se bada dushman apna dil hai — usay jeeto.

🔑 Ahm Nuktaat

Psychology = sabse badi skill — ye koi nahi sikhaata.

Journal = apna teacher — rozana baat karo.

Patience + Discipline + Consistency = Success ka formula.
