باب ۱: Trading Psychology کیا ہے؟

۱. Psychology ka matlab

English: Trading Psychology

Trading mein jazbaat ko control karna — fear, greed, overconfidence, revenge — ye sab emotions hain jo paise le jate hain.

۲. Kyun zaroori hai

80% traders paise isliye gavate hain kyunki wo emotions se trade karte hain — rules nahi follow karte. Strategy 20% hai — psychology 80%.

Misal: Aap ke paas 60% win rate hai. Magar jab loss hota hai — aap SL hata lete hain — 5% loss ho jata hai. Ek trade ne 10 profitable trades ka profit kha liya.

۴. Professional Mindset

Professional traders — emotions ko manage karte hain — rules follow karte hain — patient hote hain — consistent hote hain.

🔑 Ahm Nuktaat

Psychology > Strategy — pehle apni soch badlein.

Journal likhein — emotions ko pehchanein.

Har loss se seekhein — gusse mein trade na karein.
