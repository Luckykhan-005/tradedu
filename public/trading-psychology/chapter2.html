باب ۲: Fear & Greed

۱. Fear kya hai?

English: Fear in Trading

Nuksaan ka darr — jab price neeche jaaye to ghabra jaana — SL se pehle hi band karna — opportunity miss hona.

۲. Fear ke Types

TypeEffectSolution
Fear of LossSL pehle hi bandSL lagao — chhod do
Fear of Missing OutFOMO entryWait for setup
Fear of Being WrongOverthinkingRules follow

۳. Greed kya hai?

English: Greed in Trading

Zyada kamane ka lalach — bigger lot — wider TP — revenge trade — sab se khatarnak emotion.

۴. Greed Examples

10% profit pe bhi nahi nikalna — "aur hoga" soch ke — phir loss ho jana. Ek trade mein sara paisa lagana — "yeh sure hai" keh ke.

۵. Balance

Fear aur greed dono khatarnak hain — balanced approach chahiye — rules se dono control hotay hain.

🔑 Ahm Nuktaat

Fear = SL hatao mat — greed = size barhao mat.

Dono emotions jab aayein — 1 minute ruk jayen — rules dekhein.

Journal mein emotions note karein.
