باب ۵: Overconfidence

۱. Overconfidence kya hai?

English: Overconfidence Bias

Kuch profitable trades ke baad lagna — "mujhe aata hai" — phir bigger lot — phir loss — sab wapas chala jaata hai.

۲. Signs

1. Size barhana
2. Rules todna
3. SL hataana
4. "Ye sure hai" kehna
5. Overtrading
6. Naye markets mein bina seekhe trade

۳. Reality Check

10 profitable trades ke baad — 1 bad trade sab le jaata hai. Market hamesha sikhaata hai — koi expert nahi hai.

۴. Solution

Hamesha same size — rules kabhi na todo — profit ke baad bhi same plan — journal mein "confident" emotion note karo.

🔑 Ahm Nuktaat

Profit ke baad sabse zyada careful raho — overconfidence sabse khatarnak hai.

Same size, same rules — hamesha.

Market ko kabhi underestimate mat karo.
