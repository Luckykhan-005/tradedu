باب ۷: Trading Routine

۱. Routine kyun zaroori?

Bina routine ke trading = gambling. Routine se discipline banta hai — emotions control hotay hain — consistent results aate hain.

۲. Daily Routine

وقتعمل
Subah 8:00News check — global markets
8:30Daily + 4H chart analyze
9:00Key levels draw karein
12:00-5:00Active trading window
5:00Close all positions
6:00Journal likhein — review
RaatNext day plan banayein

۳. Weekly Routine

Saturday: Review + Backtest | Sunday: Plan next week — levels mark karein

۴. Monthly Routine

Performance review — stats calculate — strategy adjust — goals set for next month.

🔑 Ahm Nuktaat

Routine = success ka formula — ye apnao.

Subah ka analysis — raat ka planning — dono zaroori hain.

Ek din bhi routine na todo — 21 din se habit banega.
