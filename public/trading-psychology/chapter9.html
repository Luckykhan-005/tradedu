باب ۹: Journaling

۱. Journal kya hai?

English: Trading Journal

Har trade ka record — date, pair, entry, SL, TP, result, notes, emotion. Ye aapka sabse bada teacher hai.

۲. Journal Template

FieldExample
Date2026-09-20
PairBTC/USDT
DirectionLong
Entry$28,000
SL$27,500
TP$29,000
Result+$200
EmotionConfident
NotesGood setup — RSI + S/R

۳. Benefits

1. Patterns discover karein — kya kaam karta hai
2. Mistakes repeat hone se roke
3. Improvement track karein
4. Confidence badhe
5. Accountability

۴. Kaise likhein?

Excel sheet — Notion — Google Sheets — ya simple notebook. Rozana 10 minute lagao — results aayenge.

🔑 Ahm Nuktaat

Journal = success ka 50% — ye lagao.

Roz 10 minute — zindagi badal dega.

Patterns dhundho — "ye kaam karta hai" pe focus karo.
