باب ۶: Discipline

۱. Discipline kya hai?

English: Trading Discipline

Apne rules ko har haal mein follow karna — chahe loss ho, chahe profit ho — rules same rehte hain.

۲. Professional vs Amateur

ProfessionalAmateur
Rules follow karta haiRules todta hai
SL lagata haiSL hataata hai
Plan se tradeImpulse se trade
Journal likhta haiJournal nahi
PatientHasty

۳. Discipline kaise banayein?

1. Rules likhein — wall pe lagayein
2. Checklist banayein — har trade se pehle check
3. Journal likhein — daily
4. Accountability partner — kisi ko batayein

۴. Habit Building

21 din tak same routine — phir habit ban jaata hai — 90 din tak = permanent habit.

🔑 Ahm Nuktaat

Discipline sabse badi skill hai — ye sikhna padta hai.

Rules paper pe likho — wall pe lagao — daily padho.

Ek din bhi rules na todo — consistency is key.
