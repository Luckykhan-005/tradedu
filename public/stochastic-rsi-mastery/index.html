Chapter 1

Introduction: Stochastic RSI Kya Hai

Stochastic RSI ek momentum oscillator hai jo RSI se bhi zyada sensitive hota hai. Yeh RSI ke values pe Stochastic formula apply karta hai, is liye yeh short-term overbought aur oversold conditions ko jaldi pakad leta hai.

Normal RSI aksar slow rehta hai. Stochastic RSI usko refine karke faster signals deta hai. Is liye yeh short-term timing ke liye bohot useful tool hai – khas tor pe jab aap already trend ya support/resistance zone identify kar chuke hon.

Is book mein aap seekhenge:

  • Stochastic RSI aur normal RSI mein asali farq
  • Sahi settings aur levels
  • Crossovers aur divergence ka use
  • Trend ke saath timing kaise improve karein
  • Price Action ke saath confluence
  • Complete practical workflow
Stochastic RSI ek timing tool hai, direction tool nahi. Pehle bias decide karo, phir isse entry ka time refine karo.
Key Takeaway: Stochastic RSI = Faster, more sensitive version of RSI for short-term timing.

Chapter 2

RSI vs Stochastic RSI – Farq Kya Hai

Normal RSI:

  • Price changes ki speed measure karta hai
  • 0 se 100 ke scale pe
  • 70/30 classic overbought/oversold
  • Relatively smoother aur slow

Stochastic RSI:

  • RSI ke values pe Stochastic formula apply hota hai
  • Yeh bhi 0 se 100 (ya 0 se 1) scale pe hota hai
  • Bohot jaldi extreme levels pe pahunch jata hai
  • Short-term turns pehle dikhata hai
Figure 1: Stochastic RSI normal RSI se zyada aggressively move karta hai
Key Takeaway: RSI = Bigger picture momentum. Stochastic RSI = Fine-tuned short-term timing.

Stochastic RSI ka Formula aur Settings

Stochastic RSI basically yeh calculate karta hai ke current RSI value apne recent high-low range mein kahan hai.

Common Settings (Crypto ke liye):

  • RSI Length: 14
  • Stochastic Length: 14
  • K Period: 3
  • D Period: 3

Yeh settings balanced hain – na itne slow ke late signals aayein, na itne fast ke bohot noise ho.

Chapter 4

Overbought aur Oversold Levels

Stochastic RSI mein common levels:

  • Above 80 → Overbought zone
  • Below 20 → Oversold zone

Kuch traders 70/30 bhi use karte hain, lekin 80/20 zyada common hai kyunki Stochastic RSI aggressive hota hai.

Important: Overbought ka matlab yeh nahi ke turant short lo. Strong uptrend mein Stochastic RSI lambay arse tak 80 ke upar reh sakta hai. Isi tarah strong downtrend mein 20 ke neeche.

Extreme levels = Attention. Lekin direction ke liye pehle trend dekho.
Key Takeaway: 80 = Overbought attention. 20 = Oversold attention. Trend context zaroori hai.

Chapter 5

Bullish aur Bearish Crossovers

Stochastic RSI mein do lines hoti hain: %K aur %D.

  • Bullish Crossover: %K line %D line ke upar cross kare (khas tor pe oversold zone se)
  • Bearish Crossover: %K line %D line ke neeche cross kare (khas tor pe overbought zone se)

Sab se behtar crossovers woh hote hain jo extreme zones (20 ke neeche ya 80 ke upar) se aate hain.

High-Quality Bullish Signal

  • Stochastic RSI 20 ke neeche tha
  • %K ne %D ko upar cross kiya
  • Price bhi support ya demand zone pe hai
Key Takeaway: Extreme zone se crossover + Price Action confirmation = Better signal.

Chapter 6

Divergence ke Saath Stochastic RSI

Divergence tab banti hai jab price ek cheez kare aur Stochastic RSI doosri.

  • Bullish Divergence: Price lower low banaye, Stochastic RSI higher low
  • Bearish Divergence: Price higher high banaye, Stochastic RSI lower high

Divergence strong reversal signal ho sakti hai, lekin isko bhi trend aur support/resistance ke context mein dekhna chahiye.

Key Takeaway: Divergence = Warning signal. Confirmation ke baghair blindly trade mat karo.

Entry, Stop Loss aur Target

Entry: Stochastic RSI crossover + Price Action confirmation ke baad.

Stop: Recent swing low/high ke beyond ya zone ke bahar.

Target: Next resistance/support ya previous swing. Partial profits possible.

Risk 0.5–1% rakho. Agar stop bohot wide hai to position size adjust karo.
