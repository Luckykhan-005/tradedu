باب ۶: رسک مینجمنٹ

۱. 1% Rule

English: Risk Management

Har trade mein sirf 1% account ka risk. Ye rule #1 hai — kabhi na todo.

۲. Position Size Formula

Size = (Account × 1%) / SL Distance

Misal: Account $10,000 — SL $200 — Size = $100/$200 = 0.5 lot.

۳. Risk-Reward

Minimum 1:2 RR. Har trade mein. 1% risk — 2% reward. Is tarah 50% win rate pe bhi profit.

۴. Limits

Daily: 3% max loss | Weekly: 5% | Monthly: 10% — ye limits cross = break.

🔑 Ahm Nuktaat

Risk management > entry strategy.

Pehle bachein — phir kamayein.

SL hamesha lagao — kabhi hatao mat.
