باب ۱۲: مشق اور تجربہ

۱. Paper Trading

English: Paper Trading

Virtual paisa se trading karein — real feel aaye — magar loss nahi hoga. 3 mahine minimum.

۲. Backtesting

6 mahine ka chart lein — 100 setups check karein — stats nikalein. TradingView replay mode best hai.

۳. Forward Testing

Small amount se live trading — real emotions experience karein — 3 months minimum.

۴. Common Mistakes

  • Strategy hopping — bar bar badalna
  • Rules not following
  • Overtrading
  • Revenge trading
  • No journal
  • Impatience

۵. Timeline

TimeKya karein
Month 1-2Basics seekhein
Month 3-4Strategy banayein
Month 5-6Paper trading
Month 7-9Backtesting
Month 10-12Small live account
Year 2+Scale up gradually

🔑 Ahm Nuktaat

Ek saal minimum lagta hai — jaldi mat karo.

Har galti se seekho — journal mein likho.

Patient raho — results aayenge.
