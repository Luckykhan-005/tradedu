باب ۹: ٹریڈنگ پلان

۱. Trading Plan kya hai?

English: Trading Plan

Ek written document jismein ho — kaise trade karoge, kab karoge, kitna risk loge. Bina plan = gambling.

۲. Plan Template

#Section
۱Goals (monthly/annual)
۲Markets (kaun se)
۳Strategy (kaise)
۴Risk rules (1%, limits)
۵Entry rules (checklist)
۶Exit rules (SL, TP)
۷Routine (daily/weekly)

۳. Write it down

Sirf yaad rakhna kaafi nahi hai. Likho. Wall pe lagao. Daily padho.

۴. Review & Update

Monthly plan ko review karo — kya kaam kiya — kya nahi — update karo.

🔑 Ahm Nuktaat

Plan = roadmap — bina roadmap ke kahin nahi pahunchoge.

Likha hua plan — yaad rakhne se 10x better.

Plan badlo jab strategy improve ho — random mat badlo.
