باب ۷: ٹریڈنگ سائیکولوجی

۱. Emotions

Fear, greed, overconfidence, revenge — ye sab emotions hain jo account le jate hain.

۲. Fear

SL se pehle band karna. Setup aane ke baad trade na lena. Darr ke maare chhota lot.

Solution: Rules likho — SL lagao — chhod do. Trust the process.

۳. Greed

Zyada kamane ka lalach. Bada lot. SL hata dena. TP na lena.

Solution: 1% rule. TP pehle se set karo. Partial profits lo.

۴. Revenge Trading

Loss ke baad gusse mein trade. Aur loss. Cycle chalta rehta hai.

Solution: 2 loss ke baad break. Aaj ka kaam kal kar lena.

🔑 Ahm Nuktaat

Psychology 80% hai — strategy 20%.

Journal likho — emotions pehchano.

Rules sab kuch solve karte hain.
