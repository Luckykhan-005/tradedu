باب ۱: ٹریڈنگ کیا ہے؟

۱. Trading ka matlab

English: What is Trading?

Chizon ko khareedna aur bechna — price ke badlaav se faida uthana. Stock, currency, crypto, commodity — kuch bhi ho sakta hai.

۲. Trading vs Investing

TradingInvesting
Short term (minutes to weeks)Long term (months to years)
Frequent buying/sellingBuy and hold
Technical analysisFundamental analysis
Active managementPassive management

۳. Markets available

Crypto (24/7), Forex (24/5), Stocks (market hours), Commodities, Indices — har market ka apna time hai.

۴. Reality

90% traders lose money. Sirf 10% consistently profit mein hain. Wo kya karte hain jo 90% nahi karte? Rules, discipline, psychology.

🔑 Ahm Nuktaat

Trading ek skill hai — sikhna padta hai.

Bina seekhe trade = gambling.

Time lagta hai — months nahi — saal.
