باب ۴: ٹیکنیکل اینالسس

۱. Support & Resistance

English: S/R Levels

Woh levels jahan price rukti hai — buy/sell ka pressure hota hai. Ye zones hain — lines nahi.

۲. Trendlines

Upar ja raha = bullish trendline. Neeche ja raha = bearish trendline. Trend ke saath trade karein.

۳. Indicators

IndicatorUse
EMA 9/21Trend direction
RSIOverbought/Oversold
MACDMomentum
BollingerVolatility

۴. Confluence

Jab 3+ cheezein saath aayein — strong entry. Example: Trend + S/R + RSI + Volume = best entry.

🔑 Ahm Nuktaat

Indicators help karte hain — magar alone kaam nahi karte.

Confluence sab kuch hai — akela indicator mat dekhein.

Simple is better — 2-3 indicators kaafi hain.
