باب ۱۱: Journaling

۱. Journal kya hai?

English: Trading Journal

Har trade ka complete record. Ye aapka sabse bada teacher hai. Bina journal ke progress namumkin hai.

۲. Journal Template

FieldPurpose
Date + TimePattern dhundhne ke liye
Pair + DirectionKya kaam karta hai
Entry + SL + TPRules follow kiye?
ResultWin/Loss/BE
EmotionPsychology track
ScreenshotVisual record
NotesLessons learned

۳. Monthly Stats

Win Rate, Profit Factor, Max Drawdown, Avg RR — ye sab calculate karein — improvement track karein.

۴. Benefits

Patterns discover, mistakes repeat hone se rok, improvement track, accountability, confidence badhta hai.

🔑 Ahm Nuktaat

Journal = success ka 50%.

Roz 10 minutes — zindagi badal dega.

Screenshots zaroor lagao — visual learning best hai.
