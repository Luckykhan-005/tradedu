باب ۵: فنڈامنٹل اینالسس

۱. Fundamental Analysis kya hai?

English: Fundamental Analysis

Kisi cheez ki asli value dekhna — news, data, events se price ka analyze karna.

۲. Forex ke liye

DataEffect
Interest RateUSD strength
Non-Farm PayrollUSD move
CPI (Inflation)All currencies
GDPEconomy strength

۳. Crypto ke liye

Bitcoin Halving, ETF approval, regulation news, whale movements — ye sab crypto ko move karte hain.

۴. Stocks ke liye

Quarterly earnings, P/E ratio, company news, sector performance.

🔑 Ahm Nuktaat

Technical alone kaafi nahi — fundamental bhi dekhein.

News se pehle plan banayein — news ke baad nahi.

High impact news ke waqt trade na karein.
