باب ۲: ٹریڈنگ کی اقسام

۱. Scalping

English: Scalping

1-5 minute ke trades. Chhota profit, zyada trades. Professional traders ke liye.

۲. Day Trading

English: Day Trading

1-4 ghante. Din mein sab close. Beginners ke liye achha.

۳. Swing Trading

English: Swing Trading

1-7 din. Bade moves capture. Busy logon ke liye best.

۴. Position Trading

English: Position Trading

Hafte mahine. Long term vision. Fundamental analysis zaroori.
