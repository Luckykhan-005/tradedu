Introduction: Fibonacci Retracement Kya Hai

Fibonacci Retracement ek tool hai jo strong move ke baad potential pullback zones identify karta hai. Jab price ek clear swing high se swing low (ya ulta) move karti hai, to Fibonacci levels un points ke beech mein possible reaction areas dikhate hain.

Crypto market mein trends ke baad pullbacks common hote hain. 0.5 aur 0.618 levels in pullbacks mein bohot baar respect kiye jate hain. Is liye yeh levels high-probability entry zones banate hain.

Chapter 3

0.5 aur 0.618 Kyun Sab Se Important Hain

0.5 (50% Retracement)

  • Psychological halfway point
  • Bohot se traders yahan se reaction expect karte hain
  • Strong trends mein pehla solid pullback zone

0.618 (Golden Ratio)

  • Sab se famous Fibonacci level
  • Institutions aur algorithms bhi is level ko watch karte hain
  • Deep lekin still healthy pullback – trend continue hone ka achha chance

Agar price 0.786 se bhi gehra pullback kar le to trend weak ho sakta hai ya reverse ho sakta hai. Is liye 0.5–0.618 zone “sweet zone” maana jata hai.

Crypto mein 0.5 aur 0.618 pe bounce ya rejection dekhna common hai, khas tor pe jab yeh levels kisi Support/Resistance ya Order Block ke saath overlap karein.
Key Takeaway: 0.5–0.618 = Sweet zone for pullback entries in trending markets.

Chapter 4

Fibonacci Tool Ko Sahi Tarike Se Draw Karna

Uptrend (Pullback buy setup):

  • Recent clear Swing Low se start karo (0)
  • Swing High tak le jao (1)
  • Levels automatically ban jayenge

Downtrend (Pullback sell setup):

  • Recent clear Swing High se start karo (0)
  • Swing Low tak le jao (1)

Important tips:

  • Clear aur significant swings use karo (chhoti noise swings nahi)
  • Higher timeframe pe pehle major swing identify karo
  • Wick ke extreme se body ke qareeb tak flexibility rakh sakte ho, lekin consistent raho
Galat swing choose karne se poora Fibonacci unreliable ho jata hai. Pehle clear structure dekho, phir tool lagao.
Key Takeaway: Tool tabhi kaam karta hai jab aap sahi swing points choose karo.

Chapter 9

Entry, Stop Loss aur Target Planning

Entry: 0.5 ya 0.618 zone ke andar confirmation candle ke baad.

Stop: 0.786 ke thoda bahar ya recent structure ke beyond.

Targets:

  • Pehla: Previous swing high/low
  • Doosra: Fibonacci extension (1.272 / 1.618) agar trend strong ho

Risk-reward kam az kam 1:1.5 prefer karo. Agar stop wide hai aur pehla target qareeb hai to setup skip karna better hai.

Key Takeaway: Clear invalidation (stop) ke baghair Fibonacci trade mat lo.
