01

Bunyadi Terminology

Aage barhne se pehle chand basic terms clear kar lete hain jo pori book mein baar baar use hongi.

Spot Trading kya hai?

Spot trading ka matlab hai ap koi bhi crypto asset (jaise Bitcoin, Ethereum) us ki current market price par khareed rahe hain aur asset seedha apki wallet/account mein aa jata hai. Ap sirf utna hi trade kar sakte hain jitni apki apni raqam (capital) ho — koi udhar (borrowed) paisa is mein shamil nahi hota.

Futures Trading kya hai?

Futures trading mein ap asset khareedte ya bechte nahi, balke price k future movement par ek "contract" lete hain. Yahan ap leverage use kar k apni asal raqam se zyada bara position khol sakte hain — jis se profit aur loss dono amplify (barh) jate hain.

SPOT VS FUTURES — FARQ EK NAZAR MEIN
Asset ownershipSpot: Haan  |  Futures: Nahi
Leverage availableSpot: Nahi  |  Futures: Haan
Short (price girne par profit)Spot: Mushkil  |  Futures: Aasan
Liquidation ka khatraSpot: Nahi  |  Futures: Haan

Zaroori Lafz jo Baar Baar Aayenge

  • Entry Price — Jis price par ap position khole (buy ya sell).
  • Exit Price — Jis price par ap position band karein.
  • Quantity — Kitni asset units ap trade kar rahe hain.
  • Margin — Wo raqam jo ap futures trade k liye "collateral" ki tarah lagate hain.
  • Notional Value / Position Size — Trade ki total value (margin × leverage).
  • P&L — Profit and Loss.
  • ROI — Return on Investment, percentage mein.

Spot trading mein leverage nahi hoti — jo raqam apke paas hai, sirf utna hi khareed sakte hain.

Q2. Futures trading mein price girne par profit kamana...

Futures mein "Short" position lekar price girne par bhi profit kamaya ja sakta hai.

02

Spot Trading Calculations

Ye chapter is book ki bunyad hai. Yahan ap seekhenge k buy karte waqt quantity kaise nikalte hain, average price kaise calculate hoti hai, aur sell k time profit/loss kis formula se book hota hai.

2.1 — Buy karte waqt Quantity Calculate Karna

FORMULA
Quantity = Investment Amount ÷ Buy Price Yani jitni raqam laga rahe hain, use buy price se divide kar den — jawab quantity hoga.
MISAAL
Investment Amount$500
BTC Buy Price$50,000
Quantity500 ÷ 50,000 = 0.01 BTC

2.2 — Average Buy Price (Jab Multiple Baar Buy Karein — DCA)

Agar ap ek hi coin ko alag alag price par, alag alag waqt par khareedte hain (isko DCA — Dollar Cost Averaging kehte hain), tou apka average buy price yun nikalta hai:

FORMULA
Average Buy Price = Total Invested Amount ÷ Total Quantity Held
MISAAL — 2 ALAG BUY ORDERS
1st Buy0.01 BTC @ $50,000 = $500
2nd Buy0.02 BTC @ $44,000 = $880
Total Invested$1,380
Total Quantity0.03 BTC
Average Buy Price1,380 ÷ 0.03 = $46,000

2.3 — Trading Fee ka Hisaab

Har exchange buy aur sell dono par ek chhoti si fee (%) charge karta hai. Ye percentage exchange se exchange alag hoti hai, isliye apni exchange ki exact fee zaroor check karein.

Bilkul. Isko bohat simple tareeqay se samajhte hain:

% Ka Matlab Kya Hai?

% ka matlab hota hai "100 mein se"

Is liye:

DECIMAL CONVERSION
0.01% = 0.01 ÷ 100 = 0.0001

Ab 500 USDT Ka Example Dekhte Hain

Aapke paas:

  • Trade Value = 500 USDT
  • Fee = 0.01%
STEP-BY-STEP CALCULATION
Fee = Trade Value × Fee Rate Fee = 500 × 0.0001 Fee = 0.05 USDT

Yani 500 USDT ki trade par fee = 0.05 USDT.

Ek Aur Easy Shortcut

Agar fee 0.01% hai, to iska matlab hai:

Har 100 USDT par 0.01 USDT fee

SHORTCUT METHOD
500 ÷ 100 = 5 5 × 0.01 = 0.05 USDT Same answer: 500 USDT → 0.05 USDT fee

Ab Buy + Sell Dono Ki Fee Samjho

Suppose aap BUY = 500 USDT karte ho (fee = 0.05 USDT), phir price barhne ke baad SELL = 550 USDT karte ho:

Sell Fee550 × 0.0001 = $0.055
Total Fees0.05 + 0.055 = $0.105
Profit (before fees)$50
Net Profit50 − 0.105 = $49.895

Fee = Trade Amount × (Fee % ÷ 100) Example: 500 × (0.01 ÷ 100) = 500 × 0.0001 = 0.05 USDT

FORMULA
Sell Value = Quantity × Sell Price Net Profit/Loss = (Sell Price − Buy Price) × Quantity − (Buy Fee + Sell Fee) Profit/Loss % = (Net Profit ÷ Total Investment) × 100

Quantity0.03 BTC
Total Investment$1,380
Sell Price$50,000
Sell Value0.03 × 50,000 = $1,500
Fees (0.1% buy + 0.1% sell, approx.)≈ $2.88
Net Profit1,500 − 1,380 − 2.88 ≈ +$117.12
Profit %117.12 ÷ 1,380 × 100 ≈ +8.5%
MISAAL — LOSS WALA TRADE
Buy Price$46,000
Quantity0.03 BTC
Total Investment$1,380
Sell Price$42,000
Sell Value0.03 × 42,000 = $1,260
Net Loss1,260 − 1,380 ≈ −$120
Loss %−120 ÷ 1,380 × 100 ≈ −8.7%

2.5 — Break-even Price (Wo Price Jahan Na Profit Na Loss)

Break-even price woh price hoti hai jahan aap trade close karne par na profit mein hon, na loss mein. Lekin ek important baat: trading fee ki wajah se BEP aapki entry price se thori upar hoti hai (agar aap BUY/long trade mein ho).

Example Dekhte Hain

Maan lo:

  • Aapne BTC BUY kiya = $100
  • Exchange fee = 0.01%
  • Aap baad mein BTC SELL karoge

Agar BTC exactly $100 par sell kar diya, to aap break-even par nahi ho, kyunki buy fee bhi lagi aur sell fee bhi lagegi. Isliye selling price ko thora $100 se upar jana hoga.

Step 1 — Buy Fee

BUY FEE
$100 × 0.01% 0.01% = 0.0001 100 × 0.0001 = $0.01 Buy par fee = $0.01

Step 2 — Sell Fee

Agar selling price bhi $100 ho:

SELL FEE
$100 × 0.0001 = $0.01 Total fees: $0.01 + $0.01 = $0.02 Isliye $100 par sell karne se $0.02 loss hoga.

Step 3 — Break-even Price

Ab humein aisi selling price chahiye jahan selling ke baad total amount exactly original $100 ban jaye:

APPROXIMATION
$100 + $0.02 = $100.02 Yani BEP approximately: $100.02

Lekin exact calculation thori different hoti hai kyunki sell fee selling value par calculate hoti hai:

EXACT FORMULA
BEP = Entry × (1 + Fee%) ÷ (1 − Fee%) BEP = 100 × (1 + 0.0001) ÷ (1 − 0.0001) BEP ≈ $100.020002 So practically: $100.02

Simplified Formula (Zyada Tar Kaam Aayega)

SIMPLIFIED FORMULA
Break-even Price ≈ Buy Price × (1 + Buy Fee % + Sell Fee %) Yani fees jitni zyada hongi, break-even price utna hi buy price se upar chala jayega.

Real Examples — Ab Khud Try Karein

EXAMPLE 1 — 2,500 USDT ENTRY
Entry2,500 USDT
Fee0.01%
Buy + Sell Fee Impact≈ 0.02%
Fee Calculation2,500 × 0.02% = 0.50 USDT
Break-even ≈2,500 + 0.50 = 2,500.50 USDT ✅
EXAMPLE 2 — 500 USDT ENTRY, 0.02% FEE
Entry500 USDT
Fee0.02%
Buy + Sell Fee ≈0.04%
Fee Calculation500 × 0.04% = 0.20 USDT
Break-even ≈500.20 USDT ✅

Ab Thora Interesting Test — Net Profit Calculate Karein

Maan lo:

  • Entry = 500 USDT
  • Fee = 0.02%
  • Aap SELL karte ho = 502 USDT

Sawal: Net profit kitna bachega, dono fees minus karne ke baad?

SOLUTION — STEP BY STEP
Buy Price500 USDT
Sell Price502 USDT
Gross Profit502 − 500 = 2 USDT
Buy Fee (500 × 0.0002)0.10 USDT
Sell Fee (502 × 0.0002)0.1004 USDT
Total Fee0.10 + 0.1004 = 0.2004 USDT
Net Profit2 − 0.2004 = 1.80 USDT ✅

Note: 501.7996 USDT woh amount hai jo fees deduct hone ke baad sell proceeds mein bachega.

03

Futures Trading Basics

Ab hum futures trading ki bunyadi calculations ki taraf barhte hain — lekin pehle concept clear karna zaroori hai.

3.1 — Long vs Short

  • Long Position — Ap "buy" karte hain ye soch kar k price uper jayegi. Price uper jaye tou profit.
  • Short Position — Ap "sell" karte hain (bina asset rakhe) ye soch kar k price neeche jayegi. Price neeche jaye tou profit.

3.2 — Margin Types

  • Isolated Margin — Sirf utni hi raqam risk par hoti hai jitni apne us specific trade mein lagayi hai. Agar loss zyada ho jaye tou sirf wo margin khatam hoti hai, baqi account safe rehta hai.
  • Cross Margin — Apka pura account balance ek "shared pool" ki tarah us trade ko support karta hai, jis se liquidation ka khatra kam hota hai lekin loss ki soorat mein zyada raqam khatray mein aa sakti hai.
⚠ Beginners k liye generally Isolated margin behtar samjha jata hai kyunke risk ek trade tak mehdood rehta hai.
CHAPTER 03 · PRACTICE

Q1. Price girne par profit kamane k liye kaunsi position lengey?

Short position mein price girne par profit hota hai.

Q2. Isolated margin ka faida kya hai?

Isolated margin mein sirf allocate ki gayi margin hi risk par hoti hai.

04

Leverage Samajhein

Leverage futures trading ka sab se powerful — aur sab se khatarnak — tool hai. Is chapter mein ye samjhenge k leverage kaam kaise karti hai aur profit/loss par iska exact asar kya parta hai.

4.1 — Position Size (Notional Value)

Position Size / Notional Value kya hoti hai?

Notional Value ka matlab hai:

→ Aapki futures position ki total market value, chahe aapne us position ko open karne ke liye kitna margin use kiya ho.

Basic Formula

FORMULA
Position Size (Notional Value) = Entry Price × Quantity
EXAMPLE
BTC Entry$60,000
Quantity0.10 BTC

Calculation:

$60,000 × 0.10 = $6,000

Yani aapki position ki Notional Value = $6,000.

2. Leverage ka role

Leverage directly notional value calculate nahi karti.

Leverage mainly yeh batati hai ke kitna margin required hoga.

FORMULA
Required Margin = Notional Value ÷ Leverage
EXAMPLE
Position$6,000
Leverage10x
$6,000 ÷ 10 = $600

Yani approximately $600 margin chahiye.

SUMMARY TABLE
BTC Price$60,000
Quantity0.10 BTC
Notional Value$6,000
Leverage10x
Required Margin$600

3. Agar margin pata ho aur position size nikalni ho

Yeh bohat useful formula hai.

FORMULA
Notional Value = Margin × Leverage
EXAMPLE
Margin$100
Leverage20x
$100 × 20 = $2,000

Aapki maximum theoretical position:

$2,000 Notional Value

Agar BTC price $60,000 hai:

FORMULA
Quantity = Notional Value ÷ BTC Price
$2,000 ÷ $60,000 = 0.03333 BTC
RESULT
Margin$100

Quantity≈ 0.0333 BTC

FORMULA
ROI % = (P&L ÷ Margin) × 100
MISAAL

P&L+$100

06

Liquidation Price

Liquidation woh price hai jahan pohanch kar apki margin loss se poori tarah khatam ho jati hai aur exchange automatically apki position band kar deta hai. Ye futures trading ka sab se important — aur sab se ziyada avoid karne wala — number hai.

⚠ Ye formulay simplified educational version hain taake concept samajh aaye. Real exchange par exact liquidation price fees, tiered maintenance margin waghera ki wajah se thora different ho sakta hai — hamesha apni exchange ka official liquidation calculator use karein.

6.1 — Liquidation Price (Isolated Margin, Simplified)

FORMULA — LONG
Liquidation Price ≈ Entry Price × (1 − 1/Leverage + Maintenance Margin Rate)
FORMULA — SHORT
Liquidation Price ≈ Entry Price × (1 + 1/Leverage − Maintenance Margin Rate)
MISAAL — LONG, 10x LEVERAGE
Entry Price$50,000
Leverage10x → 1/10 = 0.10
Maintenance Margin Rate (farz karein)0.5% = 0.005
Liquidation Price50,000 × (1 − 0.10 + 0.005) = $45,250

Yani price sirf ~9.5% neeche jaye tou 10x leverage par position liquidate ho jayegi. Yehi wajah hai k zyada leverage = liquidation price entry k bohot qareeb hoti hai.

6.2 — Isolated vs Cross Margin Liquidation

Cross margin mein pura account balance buffer ki tarah kaam karta hai, isliye liquidation price isolated k muqable door hoti hai — lekin agar liquidation ho jaye tou pura account balance khatray mein hota hai, sirf ek trade ka margin nahi.

CHAPTER 06 · PRACTICE

Q1. Leverage barhane se liquidation price entry price k...

Zyada leverage = kam price movement mein hi liquidation.

Q2. Isolated margin mein liquidation hone par kitna nuksaan hota hai?

Isolated margin ka poora point hi ye hai k risk mehdood rahe.

Risk & Position Sizing

7.1 — Risk kya hota hai?

Trading mein Risk se murad woh maximum amount hai jo aap ek trade mein Stop Loss hit hone par lose karne ke liye accept kar rahe hain.

FORMULA
Risk Amount = Account Balance × Risk %
EXAMPLE
Account$1,000
Risk1%
$1,000 × 1% = $10

Yani is trade mein aapka planned risk $10 hai.

7.2 — Risk % kitna rakhna chahiye?

7.3 — Position Sizing kya hoti hai?

Position sizing ka matlab hai:

→ Aapke risk amount aur Stop Loss distance ke basis par kitni quantity trade karni hai.
FORMULA
Position Size = Risk Amount ÷ Stop Loss Distance

Crypto mein quantity calculate karne ke liye yeh bohat useful hai.

Step 2: SL distance

$60,000 − $59,500 = $500

BTC mein aapka SL distance $500 per BTC hai.

Step 3: Quantity

$10 ÷ $500 = 0.02 BTC

Step 4: Notional Value

$60,000 × 0.02 = $1,200

Agar leverage 10x hai:

$1,200 ÷ 10 = $120 margin
FINAL RESULT

7.5 — Stop Loss door ho to position choti hogi

Suppose same account: $1,000, Risk: $10, BTC Entry: $60,000

Lekin SL: $58,000 → SL distance: $2,000

$10 ÷ $2,000 = 0.005 BTC
Notional: $60,000 × 0.005 = $300
COMPARISON
Tight SL$500 distance → 0.02 BTC
Wide SL$2,000 distance → 0.005 BTC
→ Yani: SL jitna wide, position size utni choti. Aur: SL jitna tight, position size utni bari.
⚠ Lekin tight SL sirf position ko bara karne ke liye nahi lagana chahiye. SL ko market structure ke mutabiq hona chahiye.

7.8 — Leverage barhane se risk automatically calculate nahi hota

EXAMPLE

Chahe position 5x ho, 10x ho, ya 20x ho — Notional $5,000 rehne ki wajah se approximate price-loss $100 hi hai, fees/funding/slippage ko ignore karte hue.

⚠ Lekin high leverage liquidation price ko closer la sakti hai aur available margin ko kam kar sakti hai. Isliye leverage ko sirf "kitna profit milega" wali machine samajhna dangerous hai.

7.14 — Ek professional Position Sizing workflow

Har futures trade se pehle:

10-STEP WORKFLOW

Step 2Risk % decide karein (Example: 1%)

Step 4Technical analysis se valid Stop Loss determine karein
Step 5SL distance calculate karein
Step 6Quantity calculate karein: Risk Amount ÷ SL Distance
Step 7Notional Value calculate karein: Entry × Quantity

08

Funding Rate (Perpetual Futures)

Perpetual futures contracts ki koi expiry date nahi hoti, isliye price ko spot price k qareeb rakhne k liye ek mechanism hoti hai jisay "Funding Rate" kehte hain.

8.1 — Funding Rate Kaise Kaam Karti Hai

Ye rate generally har kuch ghanton baad (commonly har 8 ghante — apni exchange par exact interval check karein) charge/pay hoti hai:

  • Agar funding rate positive ho → Longs, Shorts ko payment karte hain.
  • Agar funding rate negative ho → Shorts, Longs ko payment karte hain.
FORMULA
Funding Payment = Position Size (Notional) × Funding Rate
MISAAL
Position Size$5,000 (Long)
Funding Rate+0.01%
Payment (Long deta hai)5,000 × 0.0001 = $0.50
› Chhota sa lagta hai, lekin agar ap zyada leverage k saath, lambe arse tak position hold karte hain, tou funding rate ka total asar significant ho sakta hai — is ko apni overall P&L calculation mein zaroor shamil karein.
CHAPTER 08 · PRACTICE

Q1. Agar funding rate negative ho tou kaun kisay pay karta hai?

Negative funding mein shorts, longs ko payment karte hain.

9.1 — Compounding Formula

FORMULA
Future Value = Principal × (1 + Return %) ^ n n = kitni baar ye return repeat hua (trades ya months)
MISAAL — $1,000 PAR 5% MONTHLY RETURN, 12 MAHEENAY
Principal$1,000
Monthly Return5%
Duration12 months
Future Value1,000 × (1.05)^12 ≈ $1,796
⚠ Compounding table achi lagti hai lekin ye assume karti hai k har trade profitable hoga — reality mein losses bhi aate hain, isliye consistent risk management (Chapter 07) hi compounding ko sustainable banati hai.

Glossary

Zaroori terms ek jaga par, jaldi refer karne k liye.

Spot Trading
Current market price par asset khareedna/bechna, asal ownership k saath.
Futures Trading
Price movement par contract lena, leverage k saath, bina asset ki ownership k.
Long
Price uper jaane par profit wali position.
Short
Price neeche jaane par profit wali position.
Leverage
Apni margin se zyada bara position control karne ki suvidha (e.g. 10x, 20x).
Margin
Position khine k liye lagayi gayi apni raqam.
Notional / Position Size
Margin × Leverage se bani total trade value.
Liquidation Price
Wo price jahan loss margin ko poora khatam kar deta hai aur position force-close ho jati hai.
Isolated Margin
Risk sirf allocate ki gayi margin tak mehdood.
Cross Margin
Pura account balance us trade ko support karta hai.
ROI
Return on Investment — margin k against percentage profit/loss.
Funding Rate
Perpetual futures mein price ko spot k qareeb rakhne wali periodic payment.
Stop Loss
Wo price jahan pohanch kar trade automatically band ho jaye taake loss mehdood rahe.
Risk:Reward Ratio
Potential loss aur potential profit ka mutual ratio.
DCA (Dollar Cost Averaging)
Alag alag price par thora thora khareed kar average cost banana.
