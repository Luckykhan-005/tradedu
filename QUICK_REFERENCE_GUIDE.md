## Risk Manager Calculator Guide

### Inputs You Control
1. **Portfolio Size (USD)**: Total account balance
2. **Margin (USD)**: Capital allocated to this specific trade
3. **Leverage**: Multiplier (e.g., 20x leverage)
4. **Direction**: LONG (bullish) or SHORT (bearish)
5. **Entry Price**: Where you enter
6. **Exit Price**: Where you exit (target)

### Automatic Calculations
- **Position Size**: Margin × Leverage
- **Max Loss**: 100% of margin allocated
- **Liquidation Price**: Where position gets forcefully closed
- **Gross P&L**: Profit/Loss before costs
- **Slippage Cost**: Trading friction impact
- **Net P&L**: Final profit/loss
- **Risk to Portfolio**: % of total portfolio at risk

### Trade Probability (%)
- Likelihood of successful trade execution
- Typical range: 70-85%+

### Risk/Reward Ratio (e.g., 4.8:1)

- Higher ratios are more favorable
- Balances risk vs. potential profit
