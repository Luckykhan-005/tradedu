## Left Sidebar Navigation

The app features a vertical navigation sidebar on the left with 6 main tabs:

1. **Opportunity Radar** (Currently showing in first screenshot)
   - Icon: Radar/broadcast signal icon
   - Subtitle: "Opportunity Radar"
   - Purpose: Main dashboard showing detected alpha opportunities

2. **Opportunity Scanner**
   - Icon: Crosshair/target icon
   - Purpose: Filter and rank detected opportunities by type, strength, and risk
   - Features:
     - Search box: "Search symbol, name, narrative..."
     - Strength filter dropdown: "Opportunity Strength"
     - Filter tabs: All, Hidden Gems, Breakouts, Reversals, Smart Money, Volume Surges, Emerging, Narrative
     - Shows: "No opportunities match your filters" message when empty
     - Note: "Try widening the filter or check back after the next scan cycle. The AI only surfaces high-probability setups."

3. **Hidden Gems**
   - Icon: Diamond/gem icon
   - Title: "Hidden Gem Radar"
   - Purpose: Low and mid-cap opportunities with highest Hidden Gem scores
   - Current Status: "No hidden gems detected"
   - Description: "The AI scans low and mid-cap projects for early accumulation patterns. When a high-quality hidden gem is detected, it will appear here."

4. **Alerts**
   - Icon: Bell/notification icon
   - Title: "24/7 AI Alert System"
   - Subtitle: "High and very-high priority alerts generated by the scanner"
   - Multiple alert cards visible with trade details

5. **Market Intelligence**
   - Icon: Info/data icon
   - Title: "Market Intelligence"
   - Subtitle: "Live macro read of the crypto market — BTC dominance, sentiment, funding, narratives"
   - Displays comprehensive market metrics and narrative strength analysis

6. **Risk Calculator**
   - Icon: Calculator/grid icon
   - Title: "Risk Manager Calculator"
   - Subtitle: "Calculate position size, P&L, liquidation price, and slippage impact for perpetual futures trades"
   - Interactive trade parameter calculator
